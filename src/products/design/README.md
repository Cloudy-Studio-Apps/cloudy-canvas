# Design

_How it works. The rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md); names may still follow the pre-refactor conventions until their phase (see its Transition table)._

Figma-style files, built only from modules (`products/design/ui/modules/design-modules.ts`: `core`, `pages`, `frame`, `catalog`, `rectangle`, `group`, `mask`, `effects`, `shader-effect`, `layers`, `assets`, `plugins-tab`, …; plugins call `registerDesignModule`). Points: `designCanvas`, `designMcpTools` and `designMigrations` (`design-points.ts`) and `designEditor` (`ui/modules/design-editor-points.ts`): the shared editor points plus `leftTabs` (a left panel tab and its rail button, in the modes its `modes` lists, the default mode's when unset: `tabsForMode`, `useLeftTabs`), `propertySections` (a properties panel section for the Design or Prototype tab, shown `when(selection)` fits, by capability rather than type) and `mainMenu` (the logo's menu: engine commands, labelled and bound by the keymap, or `run({ engine, navigate })`; a group without a label is listed in the menu, labelled ones (File, Edit, View, …) open as submenus, `mainMenuSections`, and the menu scrolls past the window's height) `toolSidebars` (what the properties panel shows instead of the sections while a tool is on), `modeSidebars` (the same while a mode is on, keyed by mode) and `layerIcons` (icons of layer types no tool creates, e.g. groups). The core module gives the default mode's entry ("Design"), so the `ModeSwitcher` shows once a module adds a mode, and `useStartMode` (a session hook) opens a design in the mode its link asks for (`/design/<id>?mode=catalog`, Home's "New Catalog") or the one it was left in (`ui/stores/design-mode-store.ts`, per file). Kinds add `canGoInto(parent, parentKind)`, `layerName(el)` (`layerNameOf`), `strokable` and `imageFills` to the node base. `DesignToolOptions` extends the modules' options (`FrameToolOptions`, `RectangleToolOptions`) and plugins augment it.

The `core` module's `core.module.ts` only wires: `core.canvas.ts` (the view and the layer tree's
hooks), `core.commands.ts` (host commands and keys), `core.sections.ts` (toolbar buttons, the
properties panel's shared sections, overlays, session hooks, the Transform context menu),
`core.menu.ts` (the main menu) and `ui/styles/core-style-types.ts` (`CORE_STYLE_TYPES`, color and
stroke styles).

## CSS gradients

- **CSS gradients:** a CSS `linear-gradient`/`radial-gradient`/`conic-gradient` pasted while layers are selected becomes their fill (`pasteGradientOnto`, the canvas `pasteOnto` hook: text pasted onto the selection instead of becoming an element), and agents give one as a paint or as `fill`/`stroke`/`color` (`readPaints`, `colorPaints`, `gradient-args.ts`).

## Child hosts

- **Child hosts:** `ChildHostKind` (`core/node/child-host.kind.ts`, `childHost`) is the base of nodes made of their children (groups, booleans): it hands turns, scales and flips to them, refuses `rotation`, isn't a snap target, and `resolveFromChildren(el, children, context)` makes it from its resolved children. The core's `resolveTree` (the design's one `resolveElements`) resolves every host innermost first in one pass (a `ChildResolveContext` with `kinds`, `insideMask`, `childrenOf`), so hosts nest in any order; `hostSelectTarget` (the `selectTarget`, `enclosingHosts`) makes a click select the outermost host not yet entered (double-click enters, Ctrl/Cmd+click goes to the layer); `pruneEmptyHosts(doc, types)` removes emptied ones; `drawsChildren` false (booleans) makes the core's `treeUndrawn` (the `undrawn` hook) leave a host's descendants undrawn. `wrapNodes`/`selectionRoots` (`core/tree/wrap-nodes.ts`) put the selection into a new host.

## Strokes

- **Strokes** (Figma's, `core/node/node-stroke.ts`): nodes have `strokeAlign` (`inside`, new shapes' default; `center`, lines', vectors' and older files'; `outside`), `strokeCap` (`none`/`round`/`square`, ends and dashes), `strokeJoin`, `strokeMiterAngle` (degrees, `miterLimit` turns it into a ratio), `strokeDashes` and, on kinds with `sidedStrokes` (rectangles, frames), `strokeWeights` per side (`strokeWeight` is then the widest). `DesignNodeKind.strokeArea(el)` is the stroke's area (`STROKE_CACHE` by shape and `strokeKey`; `sidedStrokeArea`/`boxStroke` give boxes their exact band) and `strokeOf(el)` how `drawPaints` draws it (`NodeStroke`: Pixi strokes along the (dashed) path when centered, the area filled otherwise), so image strokes work. `hitTest` is `hitsShape` (kinds override it) or `hitsStroke` (what an outside stroke reaches); booleans' operands, Outline stroke and copy/paste properties use the same settings. UI: the Strokes section (`core/ui/sections/stroke/`: position, weight, the sides menu, `StrokeSettings` with dash style (solid, dashed, dotted, custom), caps, joins, miter angle and a live `StrokePreview`). Agents: `readStrokeFields`; the Figma import maps them (`fig-stroke.ts`).

## Opacity and blend modes

- **Opacity and blend modes** (Figma's): `DesignNode.blendMode` is a `LayerBlendMode` (`pass-through`, the default, or a canvas `BlendMode`) and paints have an optional `blendMode` (normal when missing, `withBlendMode`). `layerComposite` (`core/tree/`) is what a node's scope composites: the opacity of kinds whose opacity covers what they hold (`opacityCoversChildren`: frames, sections, groups; they draw their own paints opaque, `withoutLayerOpacity`) and any blend but normal/pass-through. `hasScope` (effects or a composite) is what `treeSlots(elements, kinds)` and `designEffects` make scopes for; `isolatesComposite` (`effects/composite-isolation.ts`) isolates when it blends or what it draws overlaps (or casts effects, or blends itself). UI: `BlendModeSelect` (`ui/components/panel/`, Figma's grouped menu) in the Appearance section and the `PaintPicker`'s header. Agents: `blendMode` in `create_nodes`/`update_nodes` and paints (`layerBlendMode`, `paintBlendMode`), summaries carry it. The Figma import maps layer and paint blend modes (`fig-blend-mode.ts`); effects' blend modes are left out (counted).

## Shapes

- **Shapes** (one module each, all in the toolbar's `shapes` menu): `ellipse/` (`EllipseKind`, O, hit inside the ellipse), `polygon/` (`PolygonKind`, `pointCount` sides) and `star/` (`StarKind`, `pointCount`, `innerRadius`), both `PointsShapeKind`s whose corners come from `pointsInBox` (`core/node/shape-points.ts`), each with its section, and `line/` (`LineKind`: `points` are its two ends relative to (x, y), `startCap`/`endCap` `none` or `arrow`; `boxed = false`, not resizable, `fillable = false` (the Fills and Layout sections hide), hit near the segment, its ends drag with `LineEndDrag`; `LineTool` is both the line (L) and arrow (Shift+L) tools, Shift snaps to 45°; the Line section sets the caps).
