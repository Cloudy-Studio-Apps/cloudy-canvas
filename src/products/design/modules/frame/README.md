# Frames

Frames: containers that clip their children, drawn with F (or A) and its device presets, with the Frame section.

- **Tier:** core
- **Requires:** core
- **Contributes:** kinds `FrameKind`; designCanvas: `kinds`, `options`, `tools`; designEditor: `propertySections`, `toolSidebars`, `tools`; `designMcpTools`, `designStarters` (Mobile app and Desktop landing: one iPhone 16 or Desktop frame, `ui/starters/`)
- **Points it owns:** `frameLabelTags` (`frame.points.ts`, `FrameLabelTag`, `(frame) => string | null`: words drawn after a top-level frame's name on the canvas, the first answer that isn't `null` winning; asked on every redraw of the frame, so an answer reads what's already worked out, and a module whose answer changes calls `engine.refreshElements` on those frames. Catalog's: a repeat's template page "Product · row 2 of 40", a page it made "Copy of Product")
- **MCP tools:** `add_preset_frame`, `list_frame_presets`
- **Files:** `frame.kind.ts`, `frame.draw.ts`, `frame-presets.ts`, `create-preset-frame.ts`, `frame-nodes.ts` (Frame selection, a command of the group module), `frame.mcp.ts`, `ui/` (presets sidebar, section, `FramePresetPicker`: the sizes menu, exported from `ui/index.ts` for other modules, e.g. the catalog's blank pages).

## How it works

- **Elements:** `frame/` (`FrameKind`: a container with `fillInExports` ("Show in exports" under Fills, shown while it has fills, as in Figma: `fillExportToggle` makes the core's `forExport` leave out its fills in every exported file, at the same size; pictures for looking, like thumbnails and `view_design`, pass `asShown`), `cornerRadius` and `clipsContent` ("Clip content" in the frame section, on for new frames: `contentClip(el)` is its area, and the core's `treeClips` clips its descendants to where all their clipping frames overlap); at the top of the page it shows its name above it and is hit by it too (its body is `backgroundAt`: while it holds layers and isn't selected, a press there starts a selection box, so it's picked by its name, the Layers tab or Shift+Enter; `marqueeEncloses`: a selection box picks it only when it encloses it) (never in exports: `forExport` marks the copy `exported`), followed by the words another module adds (`frameLabelTags`, which `frame.module.ts` hands to `FrameKind(tagOf)`: drawn lighter, `label.tagGap` after the name, in `label.tagColor`; `drawnFrom` is those words, so new words draw it again; the name alone is hit); its tool, F/A, places the `framePreset` size on click; while it's on, the properties panel shows `FramePresetsSidebar`: `FRAME_PRESET_GROUPS` (Phone, Tablet, Desktop, Presentation, Watch, Paper, Social media), and a preset adds a frame named after it right of the top-level content (`presetFrameRect`, `useAddPresetFrame`), zooms to it and becomes the click size) and `rectangle/` (`RectangleKind`, R).
