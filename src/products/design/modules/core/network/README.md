# Vector networks

_How it works. The rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../../../../CLAUDE.md)._

## Vector networks

- **Vector networks** (`core/network/`, Figma's model): `VectorNetwork { vertices, segments (start, end, tangentStart, tangentEnd: relative bezier handles), regions (loops of segment indices, filled; a region's optional `fills` are its own, as in Figma: missing it shows the layer's fills, empty it's unfilled and left out of the node's fill geometry) }`. `regionPaints(region, node)` is what a region shows, `hasOwnRegionFills(net)` whether any region has its own, and `regionShapes(net)` each region normalized alone by its winding rule (cached by network, as networks are replaced, not changed, and by a region's `loops` while the vertices and segments are the same objects, so a new fill reuses the shape), which vectors with region fills draw and hit-test. `setRegionFills(net, regions, fills)` gives regions their own fills (or back to the layer's with `undefined`), `regionAt(net, p)` is the topmost region under a point (unfilled ones too), every edit keeps a surviving region's fills, and agents may give a region `fills` (`readRegion`). Picked regions are `RegionPick`s (index and `regionKey`, its loops): `pickRegions` makes them, `liveRegions` finds them in the network as it is now (at the same index, or where an undo moved them). `bezier.ts` (`pointAt`, `splitAt`, `nearestT`, `bendThrough`, `flatten`), `network-curves.ts` (`segmentCurve`, `loopCurves`, …), `network-geometry.ts` (`networkBounds`, `hitSegment`, `vertexAt`, `insideRegion`), `network-faces.ts` (`networkFaces`: the areas the segments enclose, by a half-edge walk ordered by leaving angle, outer faces dropped by their sign; `faceAt`), `network-trace.ts` (`traceStrokes`, `traceRegions`), `network-chains.ts` (`strokeChains`: chains through two-segment vertices), `network-path.ts` (`regionPath`, `strokePath`, `pathToNetwork`: a normalized shape as a network, one even-odd region per outer with its holes), pure edits in `network-edits.ts` (`moveVertices`, `setTangent` (mirrored), `splitSegment`, `addVertex`, `connect` (closing a loop adds a region), `removeParts` (heals two-segment vertices), `cutSegment`, `bendSegment`, `toggleSmooth`, `toggleRegion`), `network-shapes.ts` (`rectNetwork` with rounded corners, `ellipseNetwork`, `polygonNetwork`, `polylineNetwork`) and `placeNetwork`/`scaleNetwork`. `BoxShapeKind.vectorNetwork(el)` is a shape's outline (rectangle, ellipse, polygons and stars; `null` for frames and sections). The bezier helpers live in `canvas/path/` and are re-exported here.
