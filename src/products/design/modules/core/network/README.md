# Vector networks

_How it works. The rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../../../../CLAUDE.md)._

## Vector networks

- **Vector networks** (`core/network/`, Figma's model): `VectorNetwork { vertices, segments (start, end, tangentStart, tangentEnd: relative bezier handles), regions (loops of segment indices, filled) }`. `bezier.ts` (`pointAt`, `splitAt`, `nearestT`, `bendThrough`, `flatten`), `network-curves.ts` (`segmentCurve`, `loopCurves`, …), `network-geometry.ts` (`networkBounds`, `hitSegment`, `vertexAt`, `insideRegion`), `network-faces.ts` (`networkFaces`: the areas the segments enclose, by a half-edge walk ordered by leaving angle, outer faces dropped by their sign; `faceAt`), `network-trace.ts` (`traceStrokes`, `traceRegions`), `network-chains.ts` (`strokeChains`: chains through two-segment vertices), `network-path.ts` (`regionPath`, `strokePath`, `pathToNetwork`: a normalized shape as a network, one even-odd region per outer with its holes), pure edits in `network-edits.ts` (`moveVertices`, `setTangent` (mirrored), `splitSegment`, `addVertex`, `connect` (closing a loop adds a region), `removeParts` (heals two-segment vertices), `cutSegment`, `bendSegment`, `toggleSmooth`, `toggleRegion`), `network-shapes.ts` (`rectNetwork` with rounded corners, `ellipseNetwork`, `polygonNetwork`, `polylineNetwork`) and `placeNetwork`/`scaleNetwork`. `BoxShapeKind.vectorNetwork(el)` is a shape's outline (rectangle, ellipse, polygons and stars; `null` for frames and sections). The bezier helpers live in `canvas/path/` and are re-exported here.
