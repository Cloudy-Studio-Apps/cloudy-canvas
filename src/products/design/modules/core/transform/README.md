# Turning and flipping

_How it works. The rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../../../../CLAUDE.md)._

## Turning and flipping

- **Turning and flipping** (design): nodes are `rotatable` (`fillDefaults` gives `rotation: 0`); `DesignNodeKind.turnOf(el)` is the angle the panel shows (a line's runs along its ends) and `flip(el, axis, at)` mirrors it across a line (`flipPlacement`: the far corner mirrored, the turn negated; kinds that aren't `symmetricVertically`, polygons and stars, turn half round when flipped top to bottom; vectors mirror their network, lines their ends, groups leave it to their children). A kind whose look paints can't mirror (`mirrorsOnFlip`: a text, any layer showing a picture, vectors too, an auto layout frame, whose layers then keep their places in its space) toggles `mirrored` instead of flipping its paints (a mirrored frame's name label stays readable); vector edit mode maps through it (`vectorWorldPoint`, `localOffset`, `placeNetwork` with `w`/`mirrored`) and `shapeToVector` keeps it. `flipNodes` (`core/transform/`) runs it on the selection's roots and members; the core module's `flipHorizontal` (Shift+H) and `flipVertical` (Shift+V) host commands (`useFlipCommands`), the Transform main menu and the selection's context menu offer the turns and flips, and the Position section's `RotationRow` has the angle field (each layer turns round its middle to it) and the buttons. Groups (and booleans) are never turned themselves (`GroupKind.resolveFromChildren` wraps their children's `elementBounds`; turning one turns its children). `placeNetwork` takes the node's `rotation`, so vector edits stay put; vector edit mode maps through it (`local`, `world`, `localOffset`). A turned frame clips along its turned edges (`contentClip` passes the rotation); inside another clipping frame the nearest clip alone applies. `containerAt` checks the turned area. Agents: `rotation` in `create_nodes` and `update_nodes` (turned round the corner through `kind.rotate`, `checkNodePatch`; a group refuses it), `rotate_nodes` and `flip_nodes` (`node-transform.mcp.ts`), and summaries carry `rotation`. The Figma import keeps turns and mirrors (`placementOf` in `canvas/figma`: a mirrored matrix places the box at its far corner, `mirrored`); only skewed layers are straightened or become vectors.
