# Nodes

_How it works. The rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../../../../CLAUDE.md); names may still follow the pre-refactor conventions until their phase (see its Transition table)._

## Nodes

- **Nodes** (`modules/core/node/`): every design element is a `DesignNode` (`name`, `parentId`, `visible`, `opacity`, `fills`/`strokes` as `Paint[]`, `strokeWeight`) and its kind extends `DesignNodeKind` (a `BoxKind`: skips hidden nodes when hit, fills the shared defaults, lists its image paints as `assets`; `container` for frames, `fillable` (false for lines)). The kinds form one hierarchy: `BoxShapeKind` (a shape drawn as a box: `createNode(rect, name, index, parentId, options)` and `clickSize(options)`, its defaults' `size` unless overridden, e.g. the frame's preset) is the base of `RectangleKind`, `EllipseKind`, `FrameKind` and `PointsShapeKind` (a shape outlined by `points(el)`, which gives its drawing and hit test: `PolygonKind`, `StarKind`); `LineKind` extends `DesignNodeKind` directly. A paint is a `SolidPaint`, a `GradientPaint` or an `ImagePaint` (`assetId`, `scaleMode`, Figma's: `fill` crops to cover, `fit` shows it all, `crop` shows what its `transform` (an `ImageTransform`) picks, `tile` repeats it at `scalingFactor` (default `DEFAULT_TILE_SCALE`, 0.5; the Scale tool scales it, `scaleTiles`); `rotation` turns `fill`/`fit`/`tile` by quarters; `imagePaint(assetId)`, `imagePlacement(paint)` is how it's drawn, `toCropPaint(paint, box, image)` a crop showing what it showed); the kind's `assets` are its image paints. **Cropping:** the image module's `cropImage` host command (toolbar `selectionActions` button, context menu; `useCropImageCommand`) or the paint picker's Crop (`PaintField` while open) put a fill in crop mode (`ui/stores/image-crop-store.ts`), and the image module's `ImageCrop` overlay shows the `canvas-ui` `ImageCropOverlay` over the layer (resizing the layer goes through `kind.resize`). The picker's `ImageEditor` has the four modes, Rotate 90° (`rotateTransform90` for a crop), the tile scale and a CSS preview (`imagePreviewStyle`). Agents give `crop: { x, y, w, h }` or `transform`, `scalingFactor` and `rotation` (`image-paint-args.ts`); the Figma import maps STRETCH (crop), TILE (`scale`) and `rotation` (`fig-image-paint.ts`). A `GradientPaint` is a canvas `Gradient` (`canvas/gradient/`: `type` `linear`/`radial`/`angular`/`diamond`, `stops` (`ColorStop`: position, color, opacity, optional `ease` to the next one), `transform`, Figma's 2×3 `gradientTransform` from the box's normalized space, and optional `interpolation`, `oklab` for smoother middles) plus opacity and visibility. `gradientPaint(type, from)` starts one as Figma does (the paint's color fading out, linear top to bottom, the others centered; `defaultGradientTransform`), `isGradientPaint` tells them apart, `gradientHandles`/`transformFromHandles` turn the matrix into the start, end and width handles and back, `rotateGradient90`, `flipGradient` (`flipPaints`, run by every kind's `flip`), `reverseStops`, `addStop`, `sampleStops` edit and sample them, and `gradientCss`/`parseCssGradient` go to and from CSS. Factories start from `createNodeBase`. **A node's shape is data:** `DesignNodeKind.geometry(el)` is its `NodeGeometry` (`{ fill, stroke }` `PathShape`s in local coordinates, the fill normalized so holes stay open), cached in `GEOMETRY_CACHE` (an LRU) by `type` + `geometryKey(el)` (only the fields that change the shape; a vector's is `identityKey(el.network)`, as stored networks are replaced, not changed) and worked out by `buildGeometry(el)` (`BoxShapeKind`: its `vectorNetwork` through `networkGeometry(net, normalize)`; frames and sections their rounded box; vectors their regions normalized by winding rule; lines a stroke-only path; text its glyphs). Drawing, hit tests, vector masks, booleans and Flatten all read it; `ownsShape` (false for groups) says whether a node has one of its own. Kinds `draw` with `drawPaints(view, node, geometry, kind.strokeOf(el))` (fills, consecutive solid and gradient ones in one drawing, gradients laid on the node's box, image fills clipped to the fill, then strokes, with the node's opacity and visibility, through `view.setPaints`; plugins' kinds may still pass a hand-traced `PaintOutline`); `traceBox` is a (rounded) box outline. Drawing tools extend `NodeDrawTool<E, S>` (`modules/core/tools/`, a template method: the press/drag/click gesture past `DRAW_THRESHOLD_PX`, naming ("Rectangle 3", `nextLayerName`), adding at the top, placing it in the frame under it with `parentFor` (`core/tree/parent-for.ts`), selecting it and returning to select, in one undo step); a subclass gives the shape `S` a drag or click makes (`dragShape`, `clickShape`, `createNode`, `shapePatch`). `BoxDrawTool` is the concrete tool for any `BoxShapeKind` (`new BoxDrawTool(ctx, kind)`: id from `kind.tool`, name from `kind.label`), so box shapes have no tool classes of their own; `LineTool` draws between two ends.
