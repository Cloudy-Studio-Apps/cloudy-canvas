# Catalog

Print brochures inside a design: in the Catalog mode a page's top-level frames are a brochure's pages, with spreads, margins, checks before printing and print-ready PDFs: bleed, crop marks, CMYK from a print shop's profile and PDF/X.

- **Tier:** feature
- **Requires:** core, pages, frame; uses: image, text
- **Contributes:** kinds `BrochureKind`; designCanvas: `afterEdit` (`syncPageItems`), `editElement`, `kinds`, `onRemove`, `prepareCopies` (`prepareItemCopies`); designEditor `layerBadges`, `propertySections` (master copies); the Catalog mode (`registerDesignMode`: canvas `modes`, `tools`, `hostCommands`; designEditor `modes`, `leftTabs`, `modePanels`, `overlays`, `sessionHooks`); `designMcpTools`, `designMigrations`, `designStarters` (A4 brochure: four blank A4 pages in a new brochure, opened in the Catalog mode, `ui/starters/`)
- **Points it owns:** —
- **MCP tools:** `add_brochure_pages`, `arrange_brochure`, `create_brochure`, `delete_brochure`, `duplicate_brochure`, `export_brochure_pdf`, `get_brochure`, `get_brochure_page`, `list_brochures`, `move_brochure_page`, `remove_brochure_pages`, `rename_brochure`, `set_brochure_page_options`, `set_brochure_pages`, `set_brochure_settings`, `view_brochure`
- **Files:** `brochure/` (the brochure records), `model/` (pages, spreads, arrange), `catalog.mode.ts` (the Catalog mode, one `defineDesignMode` object), `mode/` (its tools, fading and allowed commands), `checks/` (DPI, placeholders), `export/` (PDF), `mcp/`, `ui/`.

## How it works

- **Print-ready PDFs** (`export/print-options.ts` is the one place that turns `BrochureSettings` into `PdfExportOptions`): the settings add `bleed` (mm), `cropMarks`, `colorMode` (`rgb`, `cmyk`), `printProfile` (a document asset, listed by `BrochureKind.assets` so it stays in the file and copies, with its name) and `renderingIntent`, edited in `ui/sidebar/print-section.tsx`. `storePrintProfile` reads and keeps a `.icc` file, for the panel (`useLoadPrintProfile`) and agents (`printProfilePath`). Pages are drawn with `bleedFrame` (frame module), so fills and clips reach the bleed. CMYK files are PDF/X: Sharp gives X-4, keeping live transparency; Image gives X-1a, flat pages. `checks/print-issues.ts` reports CMYK with no profile, painted layers stopping on a page's edge and rounded pages, which `bleedFrame` leaves as they are. Show margins also draws the bleed.
- **Catalog mode** (`catalog/`): a design page's top-level frames as a print brochure's pages, as a mode of the same file (one source of truth). A `brochure` record (`BrochureKind`, never on a page's canvas; `name`; `page`: the design page it belongs to, whose frames only it uses (`brochurePages` skips options on another page); a page holds any number; `openedAt`: the page's open brochure is the one opened last, else its first, written untracked; `slots`: `BrochureSlot { id, frames, chosen }`, a page's options, alternatives like "Opción A/B/C", and the one printed; `settings`: `layout` `book`/`single`, `covers` `both`/`front`/`back`/`none`, `splitSpreads`, `margin` mm, `dpi`; older records' `facing` becomes the layout in `fillDefaults`) is listed, made, renamed, duplicated, deleted and opened only through `DESIGN_BROCHURES` (`DesignBrochures`: `list(page)`, `current(page)`, `get`, `create(page, name?, open?)`, `rename`, `duplicate`, `remove`, `open`, one undo step each but `open`; `brochuresOf(doc)` where there's no engine; `currentBrochure(workspace)` is the open one of the workspace's page, `ensureBrochure` makes one when there's none, what picking, adding a blank page and the canvas's "+ Page" do) and edited through the `DesignBrochure` handle it gives (`pages()` resolves slots into `BrochurePage`s, skipping ids that are gone or no longer page frames, `isPageFrame`; `addPages`, `addOption`, `choose`, `move`, `removePages`, `removeFrames`, `pick`, `setSlots`, `setSettings`, `addNewPage`, one undo step each, over the pure `slot-edits.ts`); frames are kept by id, so renames are free, and `pruneBrochure` (an `onRemove`) takes deleted frames out of every brochure and deletes a deleted page's brochures in their deletion's undo step (duplicating a page doesn't copy its brochures yet). Files from before brochures had a page are split by the page of each slot's printed frame when they open (`splitLegacyBrochure`, a `designMigrations` entry). A world unit prints as a point (`paperSize`, `mmToUnits`; Paper presets are in points). `bookLayout(pages, settings)` (`DesignBrochure.layout()`) is how it prints and is read: `sides` (`BookSide`: a page, `part` `whole` or the `left`/`right` half of a spread frame, or a `blank` added so the back cover ends on the left or a spread starts on a left page), `spreads` (sides read together; in the book layout side `i` is a left page when `i + offset` is even, `offset` 1 with a front cover, alone on the right), `labels` ("6–7"), `spreadNumbers` and `pageCount`; a spread frame is one about twice the brochure's page (`brochurePageSize`, the commonest printed size, `spanOf`, `fitsPage`). The PDF prints `printedPages` (blanks as image-less pages, spread frames split in halves with `splitSpreads`, `drawFrame(…, part)`, `partRect`). The Catalog mode (`catalogMode`, `catalog.mode.ts`): `isolate: isPageFrame` (a click inside a frame reaches it), `faded: catalogFaded` (what the page's open brochure doesn't print, read through `ModeFadeContext.scope`; nothing while picking), `commands: CATALOG_ALLOWED_COMMANDS` (it organizes, never edits); its tool is `CatalogTool` (a `SelectTool` that doesn't move or resize; Delete takes pages out, the frames stay), with the hand and `CatalogPickTool` (ticks frames as the selection, starting with the printed ones; `applyPick` keeps ticked pages, drops unticked ones of the page on screen and adds new ones in `readingOrder`); `editCatalogPage` (double-click, Enter) designs a page. **Arranging on the canvas:** `settings.arrange` (`BrochureArrange`: `off`, the default, `below`, `beside`; `BROCHURE_ARRANGES`) lays the brochure's frames out (`model/arrange/`): `arrangeBlocks(layout, settings)` turns the layout's spreads into `ArrangeBlock`s (a page `left`/`right` of the spine or `across` it), `arrangeFrames(blocks, mode, origin)` places the printed pages on one line from the frames' current top-left (`below`: spreads left to right, each page's other options stacked under it; `beside`: a spread per row on one spine, a left page's options to the left, the others' to the right; lanes as wide as their widest frame, `ARRANGE_GAPS`), `pushAside(area, obstacles, gap)` moves the page's other top-level layers (each by its own and its contents' turned bounds) up or down out of the way, cascading onto what they'd land on, and `arrangeBrochure(doc, brochure, layout, pages, mode)` writes the moves (with descendants, no checkpoint) and returns each moved node's offset. While it isn't `off`, every `DesignBrochure` edit (choosing an option swaps it onto the line, adding, removing and moving pages, settings) arranges in the same undo step; `arrange()` (the Catalog toolbar's Arrange button, the sidebar's `ArrangeFields` Arrange now, agents' `arrange_brochure`) does it once, as `below` while it's off. Entering the mode, or showing another page or brochure in it, arranges again while it's on (`keepArranged`, the `useArrangeOnEnter` hook of the mode: 150 ms after the switch shows, when the app is idle, so the mode appears at once and the frames then glide), as frames may have moved in the Design mode. Page thumbnails are `canvas-ui`'s `ElementThumbnail`s (kept while a frame only moved with all it holds, drawn when the app is idle). Moves are reported by `DesignBrochures.onArranged`, and `useArrangeGlide` (a session hook) has them glide with `engine.animateMoves`. `brochureIssues`/`checkBrochure` find blank pages the layout added and frames of another size (`layoutIssues`), hidden pages, image placeholders still to replace (`placeholder`, shown tagged in the page's Images with Replace, and as `placeholder: true` in `get_brochure_page`), pictures under `LOW_DPI` at print size (`effectiveDpi` by `scaleMode`, natural sizes from `pictureSize`, read once per asset) and text in the margin. `exportBrochure(workspace, brochure)` (and `checkBrochure`, `drawBrochureSheet`, which take the brochure too) adds each printed frame to a `PdfBook` (on its design page through a workspace, one at a time, a unit to a point), sharp or flattened as `settings.pdfMode` says, with pictures at `settings.dpi`. UI (`catalog/ui/`; `useBrochure()` is the open brochure of the page on screen, `null` when it has none): the Brochure tab (its `rail` tab: `BrochureSwitcher` atop it (the page's brochures, New brochure, Rename (also double-click), Duplicate, Delete), `NoBrochure` (Create brochure) on a page without one; rows drag to reorder (`useRowDrag`; a page dropped on a blank page takes its place, on a spread's heading starts that spread or, on the back cover's, is the back cover, and below the last row goes last; the back cover dropped on the blank added before it turns the back cover off, `dropsBackCover`, as moving pages never removes that blank: `dropPosition` over the drop ids of `dropId`/`parseDropId`, `ui/brochure-tab/drop-position.ts`; Ctrl/⌘ and Shift + click select several pages, `usePageListSelection`, whose selection is the canvas's (their frames, `selectedSlots`), and they drag together, `moveMany` over `moveSlots`; Alt+↑/↓ are the mode's `keybindings` for `catalog.movePageUp`/`movePageDown`, `moveSelectedPages`), the selected page lists its options, grouped by the layout's spreads (Front cover, Pages 2–3, Back cover) with its blank pages as rows, `spreadLabel`, `BlankPageRow`; "+" adds a blank page like the last, the empty state picks frames or starts a blank A4 page, either way, or any frame size through frame's `FramePresetPicker`), its `sidebar` (`CatalogSidebar`: settings (`LayoutFields`: layout, covers, split spreads; the kit's `SelectField`/`CheckboxField`), checks, Export PDF and Preview; a page's options and pictures with Replace; a frame not in it; several frames; picking), `BrochureCanvasLayer` (an overlay of the mode, mounted only while it's on: page badges, 9A/9B for options, "+ Page / + Option of page N" on the greyed frame under the pointer, margins, pick ticks and banner), its toolbar (`useCatalogToolbarItems`: its tools, the hand from its tool entry, the Pick/Preview/Export commands, Arrange; pick and preview are the commands' `run`, the PDF export `useExportCommand`, which reads the file's name), `BrochurePreview` (an overlay of the mode, closed when the mode is left, `onLeave`: spreads, ← →, the strip; by default a 3D booklet, `BookSpread`, tilted in perspective with page edges and the spine's shade, whose pages turn over the spine, `TurningSheet` with the Web Animations API, laid out over the layout's sides by the pure `bookSlots`/`turningBook`/`restingBook`, a side being a page, half a spread frame (`PreviewPage` `part`, the whole picture clipped) or blank paper (`SidePicture`), the single layout centering each page by its width (`stageSize`, `sideWidth`) and timed by `usePageTurn`, skipped with reduced motion; the "3D view" switch, `catalog-view-store.preview3d`, shows them flat; pictures are drawn once and kept, `previewImagesOf`, and the spreads around are drawn ahead). Agents (`catalog/agent/`: `brochure-list`, `brochure-read`, `brochure-edit`, `brochure-settings` tool files over `brochure-args.ts` and `describe-brochure.ts`; pages by their 1-based `position`; every tool but `list_brochures` and `create_brochure` requires the `brochure` id, so agents never guess one, `readBrochure`): `list_brochures`, `create_brochure` (opened only when the page had none), `rename_brochure`, `duplicate_brochure`, `delete_brochure`, `get_brochure`, `get_brochure_page` (its pictures, `pagePictures`, with size and dpi), `view_brochure` (`drawBrochureSheet`: a spread, or a contact sheet of every spread with its numbers), `set_brochure_pages`, `add_brochure_pages` (frames, or a `blank` paper frame through `addNewPage`), `move_brochure_page`, `remove_brochure_pages`, `set_brochure_page_options` (`setOptions`), `set_brochure_settings`, `export_brochure_pdf`, each edit one undo step; the image module's `replace_image` swaps any layer's picture keeping its placement (`replaceImageFill`, shared with the page pictures' Replace). Home shows a design left in the Catalog mode as a catalog (`pages/home/file-kind.ts`). **Masters, sections and numbering** (`model/masters/`, edited through `DesignBrochure.masters`, a `BrochureMasters`, one undo step each): a record's `masters` (`BrochureMaster { id, name, left, right }`: a frame for left pages and one for right pages on the brochure's design page, made the brochure's page size above the page's layers by `createMasterFrames`, transparent; never pages, `brochurePages` skips `masterFramesOn` the page; undimmed in the mode), `sections` (`BrochureSection { id, name, start, restart?, style? }`, one per starting slot, `keptSections` drops those whose page went, also in `pruneBrochure`), a slot's `master` (left out: `settings.defaultMaster`, the first master made; `null`: none) and `skip` (master layers detached on it), and `settings.numbering` (`start`, `style` `arabic`/`roman-lower`/`roman-upper`, `covers` `numbered`/`hidden`/`uncounted`) and `mastersOnCovers`. A deleted master frame leaves its side `null`, and a master with neither side goes (`prunedMasters`, `withoutMaster`). **Stamping** (`model/page-items/`): `syncPageItems`, the module's `afterEdit` hook (in the edit's undo step and transaction, never a checkpoint of its own), finds the design pages an edit touched (`affectedPages`: a brochure record, a layer inside one of a brochure's page or master frames, a copy or a page item) and, for each, the open brochure's `stampTargets` (every printed side on every option frame of its page, a spread frame's halves apart; its master by `slot.master`, `settings.defaultMaster`, the side's `parity`; covers only with `mastersOnCovers`) and `planPageItems`, a pure diff: each master layer (all it holds, but `slot.skip`) gets a copy in the page frame, moved from the master frame to the page, `locked`, tagged `catalogCopy` (`PageItemTag { brochure, master, source, half? }`, read with `pageItemTag`), matched to the copy already there by frame, source and half and patched with what changed, new ones on top of the page's layers; a text with a `catalogText` template (`pageItemTemplate`) reads it for its page (`resolveTemplate`: `{page}`, `{pages}`, `{title}`, `{section}`, `{date}` in the app's language, `{toc}` from `tableOfContents`, `{{` for a brace) and is resized keeping its aligned edge, its styled ranges dropped (`itemTextPatch`; a copy whose text didn't change isn't measured again, `copyOf`); the page's own page items (untagged texts with a template) read for the half they're on; every other copy on the page goes (a master or page no longer used, another brochure's, a repeat). Only what differs is written, so its own writes, which run it again, end it. Page numbers are `pageNumbers(layout, record)` (`PageInfo { counted, number, label, section, parity, cover }`). `{date}` is read when the brochure's pages are next edited. **In the Design mode** the copies are locked (clicks reach the page frame), marked in the layers panel (`FromMasterBadge`, a `layerBadges` entry) and shown in the properties panel (`FromMasterSection`: where they come from, Edit master, which selects the master layer, and Detach, `detachCopies`: untagged and unlocked, their sources added to the page's `skip`, one undo step); unlocking one is undone by the next sync. Copies copied with their page frame are left out of the copies, and copied alone they're freed (`prepareItemCopies`, a `prepareCopies` hook). Not yet: bleed in the PDF.
