# Catalog

Print brochures inside a design: in the Catalog mode a page's top-level frames are a brochure's pages, with spreads, margins, checks before printing and print-ready PDFs: bleed, crop marks, CMYK from a print shop's profile and PDF/X.

- **Tier:** feature
- **Requires:** core, pages, frame; uses: image, text, data
- **Contributes:** kinds `BrochureKind`; designCanvas (`catalog.canvas.ts`): `afterEdit` (`syncBrochureRepeats`, then `syncPageItems`), `editElement`, `kinds`, `onRemove`, `prepareCopies` (`prepareItemCopies`, `prepareRepeatCopies`); data's `dataScopeProviders` (`pageScopeProvider`) and `dataLocaleProviders` (`pageLocaleProvider`); designEditor `layerBadges`, `propertySections` (master copies); the Catalog mode (`registerDesignMode`: canvas `modes`, `tools`, `hostCommands`; designEditor `modes`, `leftTabs`, `modePanels`, `overlays`, `sessionHooks`); `designMcpTools`, `designMigrations`, `designStarters` (A4 brochure: four blank A4 pages in a new brochure, opened in the Catalog mode, `ui/starters/`); `catalogPageItems` (the built-ins, `BUILT_IN_PAGE_ITEMS`)
- **Points it owns:** `catalogPageItems` (`ui/catalog.points.ts`: the Page items list's entries, `PageItemEntry`, a `PageItemDefinition` with its icon)
- **MCP tools:** `add_brochure_pages`, `add_page_item`, `arrange_brochure`, `create_brochure`, `create_brochure_master`, `delete_brochure`, `detach_master_layer`, `duplicate_brochure`, `export_brochure_pdf`, `get_brochure`, `get_brochure_page`, `list_brochures`, `move_brochure_page`, `remove_brochure_master`, `remove_brochure_pages`, `rename_brochure`, `set_brochure_page_options`, `set_brochure_pages`, `set_brochure_sections`, `set_brochure_settings`, `set_page_item`, `view_brochure`
- **Files:** `brochure/` (the brochure records), `model/` (pages, spreads, arrange, repeats), `catalog.canvas.ts` (its canvas hooks and data providers), `catalog.mode.ts` (the Catalog mode, one `defineDesignMode` object), `mode/` (its tools, fading and allowed commands), `checks/` (DPI, placeholders), `export/` (PDF), `mcp/`, `ui/`.

## How it works

- **Print-ready PDFs** (`export/print-options.ts` is the one place that turns `BrochureSettings` into `PdfExportOptions`): the settings add `bleed` (mm), `cropMarks`, `colorMode` (`rgb`, `cmyk`), `printProfile` (a document asset, listed by `BrochureKind.assets` so it stays in the file and copies, with its name) and `renderingIntent`, edited in `ui/sidebar/print-section.tsx`. `storePrintProfile` reads and keeps a `.icc` file, for the panel (`useLoadPrintProfile`) and agents (`printProfilePath`). Pages are drawn with `bleedFrame` (frame module), so fills and clips reach the bleed. CMYK files are PDF/X: Sharp gives X-4, keeping live transparency; Image gives X-1a, flat pages. `checks/print-issues.ts` reports CMYK with no profile, painted layers stopping on a page's edge and rounded pages, which `bleedFrame` leaves as they are. Show margins also draws the bleed.
- **Catalog mode** (`catalog/`): a design page's top-level frames as a print brochure's pages, as a mode of the same file (one source of truth). A `brochure` record (`BrochureKind`, never on a page's canvas; `name`; `page`: the design page it belongs to, whose frames only it uses (`brochurePages` skips options on another page); a page holds any number; `openedAt`: the page's open brochure is the one opened last, else its first, written untracked; `slots`: `BrochureSlot { id, frames, chosen }`, a page's options, alternatives like "Opción A/B/C", and the one printed; `settings`: `layout` `book`/`single`, `covers` `both`/`front`/`back`/`none`, `splitSpreads`, `margin` mm, `dpi`; older records' `facing` becomes the layout in `fillDefaults`) is listed, made, renamed, duplicated, deleted and opened only through `DESIGN_BROCHURES` (`DesignBrochures`: `list(page)`, `current(page)`, `get`, `create(page, name?, open?)`, `rename`, `duplicate`, `remove`, `open`, one undo step each but `open`; `brochuresOf(doc)` where there's no engine; `currentBrochure(workspace)` is the open one of the workspace's page, `ensureBrochure` makes one when there's none, what picking, adding a blank page and the canvas's "+ Page" do) and edited through the `DesignBrochure` handle it gives (`pages()` resolves slots into `BrochurePage`s, skipping ids that are gone or no longer page frames, `isPageFrame`, in the order its repeats expand them (see Repeating sections and pages); `addPages`, `addOption`, `choose`, `move`, `removePages`, `removeFrames`, `pick`, `setSlots`, `setSettings`, `addNewPage`, one undo step each, over the pure `slot-edits.ts`); frames are kept by id, so renames are free, and `pruneBrochure` (an `onRemove`) takes deleted frames out of every brochure and deletes a deleted page's brochures in their deletion's undo step (duplicating a page doesn't copy its brochures yet). Files from before brochures had a page are split by the page of each slot's printed frame when they open (`splitLegacyBrochure`, a `designMigrations` entry). A world unit prints as a point (`paperSize`, `mmToUnits`; Paper presets are in points). `bookLayout(pages, settings)` (`DesignBrochure.layout()`) is how it prints and is read: `sides` (`BookSide`: a page, `part` `whole` or the `left`/`right` half of a spread frame, or a `blank` added so the back cover ends on the left or a spread starts on a left page), `spreads` (sides read together; in the book layout side `i` is a left page when `i + offset` is even, `offset` 1 with a front cover, alone on the right), `labels` ("6–7"), `spreadNumbers` and `pageCount`; a spread frame is one about twice the brochure's page (`brochurePageSize`, the commonest printed size, `spanOf`, `fitsPage`). The PDF prints `printedPages` (blanks as image-less pages, spread frames split in halves with `splitSpreads`, `drawFrame(…, part)`, `partRect`). The Catalog mode (`catalogMode`, `catalog.mode.ts`): `isolate: isPageFrame` (a click inside a frame reaches it), `faded: catalogFaded` (what the page's open brochure doesn't print, read through `ModeFadeContext.scope`; nothing while picking), `commands: CATALOG_ALLOWED_COMMANDS` (it organizes, never edits); its tool is `CatalogTool` (a `SelectTool` that doesn't move or resize; Delete takes pages out, the frames stay, and is called "Take out of the brochure" there, `commandLabels`; Shift+Delete, `catalog.deletePages`, also in the selection menu and a page row's menu, deletes the selected page frames from the design, `deleteSelectedPages`, one undo step, only taking out of this brochure a frame another brochure of the page prints), with the hand and `CatalogPickTool` (ticks frames as the selection, starting with the printed ones; `applyPick` keeps ticked pages, drops unticked ones of the page on screen and adds new ones in `readingOrder`); `editCatalogPage` (double-click, Enter) designs a page. **Arranging on the canvas:** `settings.arrange` (`BrochureArrange`: `off`, the default, `below`, `beside`; `BROCHURE_ARRANGES`) lays the brochure's frames out (`model/arrange/`): `arrangeBlocks(layout, settings)` turns the layout's spreads into `ArrangeBlock`s (a page `left`/`right` of the spine or `across` it), `arrangeFrames(blocks, mode, origin)` places the printed pages on one line from the frames' current top-left (`below`: spreads left to right, each page's other options stacked under it; `beside`: a spread per row on one spine, a left page's options to the left, the others' to the right; lanes as wide as their widest frame, `ARRANGE_GAPS`), `pushAside(area, obstacles, gap)` moves the page's other top-level layers (each by its own and its contents' turned bounds) up or down out of the way, cascading onto what they'd land on, and `arrangeBrochure(doc, brochure, layout, pages, mode)` writes the moves (with descendants, no checkpoint) and returns each moved node's offset. **Repeating sections and pages** (`model/repeats/`). A section's `repeat` ("Repeat for each row") or a slot's ("Repeat this page") is a `BrochureRepeat` (`source`, a list in scope such as `products` or `category.products` inside an outer repeat; `as`, `item` when empty, `DEFAULT_REPEAT_AS`; `key`, `filter`, `sort`, `limit` as a card repeater's; `designLimit`, the copies made while designing, `DEFAULT_BROCHURE_DESIGN_LIMIT` 3 unset; `previewRow`; `perPage`, pages only, rows on each copy, ≥ 1; `detached`, each detached copy's whole copy path, so a product detached in one category stays in the others). A slot a repeat made carries `generated` (`GeneratedSlot { from, path }`: its template slot and its copy path); both are optional, so older records need no default. `repeatTree(record)` reads the template slots (`templateSlots`: every slot but generated ones) as nested loops: a section holds the pages from its start to the next section at its depth or less, its subsections inside (`placeSections`, `sectionWithChildren`), and a repeating one also ends before the back cover (`backCoverSlot`: the last slot of a book with a back cover, read from the record), so a closing page prints once; `repeatRuns(record)` gives each repeat's template slot ids (a page repeat's is its one slot). `expandBrochure(record, root, phase)` walks that tree like an interpreter: at a repeating section or page it reads the rows (`repeatRows` in the export's phase, every row, over the scope of the loops around it), then yields its pages once per pass (`loopContexts`): the template pages first, at the preview row while designing (`previewRow` clamped; with several rows a page, the page holding it) and at the first row in an export, so the print follows the rows, either way the first row from there that isn't detached; the other rows as copies (`path` + `copySegment(owner, key)`, `${owner}:${encodeURIComponent(key)}` with the repeating section's or slot's id, so ids never collide across loops and a `/` in a key can't split them; a page repeat with `perPage` above 1 passes chunks of rows keyed by their position, its `as` the chunk's list, whose fields read `item[].price`, `loopNames`). A copy is `made` while its place among its loop's copies is within `designLimit` and every loop around it is made (in an export, all); the rest are pending, planned in full with their inner loops so counts and page numbers are right. An empty list (or every row detached) gives the template pages alone, `printed: false` (no copies); inside an outer copy that pass has the copy's path and no frame is made for it. A list this device can't read gives the template alone, printed (its copies elsewhere are kept, below). Detached copies give none. Every pass adds a `ScopeLayer` (data's: `name` the `as`, `value`, `owner` the section or slot id, `label` the section's name or the page repeat's source, `index`, `count`) with `names` (`loopNames`) and `unreadable` when its list reads a source this device can't read, and reads in data's `layerScope` over the loops around it (`$index`, `$number`, `$count`), the helper data layers a page's loops with. Sections are yielded per pass too (`ExpandedSection { id, from, path, name, level, start, first, end }`, `first`/`end` its pages' range in the expansion as the tree reads it, so a repeating section's stops before the back cover: the one notion of that cut, `repeatTree`'s), their names' tokens read in the pass's scope in the brochure's language (`copyName`; a token nothing answers stays as written). Ids are `expandedId(from, path)`: the template's own id, else `from@` and the path joined with `/` (`copyPathKey`). `BrochureExpansion.pending` counts the pages not made that print.

**Making the copies** (`syncBrochureRepeats`, an `afterEdit` before `syncPageItems`, so masters are stamped on new pages; in the edit's undo step). For each brochure with repeats (or copies left) whose record, template pages or sources the edit changed, it expands the brochure once (`expandBrochure` in data's `dataPhase`: an export's scratch copy makes every page) and writes what differs (`planBrochureRepeats`, `writeBrochureRepeats`; nothing when nothing does): each copy made that prints is a page frame on the brochure's design page (`planPageCopy`: every layer of its template slot's printed frame, `templateCopyParts`, so the template's master copies and card repeaters' copies are left out and their syncs make them on the page; locked and tagged `repeatCopy` (`RepeatCopyTag { brochure, iterator, key, source }`: the loop that made it, its `copyPathKey`, the template layer), matched by `source` and patched where the template changed (`pageCopyPatch`) but never in what data, a layout or the page items write (`dataResolvedPaths`, `layoutPlacedPaths`, a page item's text, whose aligned edge is kept), a card repeater inside naming its card's copy; a new page goes after the page before it, and the brochure is arranged again when its `arrange` is on), listed as a slot with `generated` (and its template slot's `master` and `skip`), the slots in the expansion's order. A copy is found by its slot, else by its tag (template frame and copy path, the first in sibling order winning, `brochureCopies`), so two clients that made the same copy end with one. A copy no longer made goes, frame and slot, and so does every copy frame of the brochure no slot lists once the sync has written them (`orphanLayers`: the loser of such a race, a slot lost to a concurrent edit); one of a list this device can't read (`ScopeLayer.unreadable`) stays as it is, after the slot before it. Undo and redo don't run the sync, and what they restore is consistent. Then the pages it made or changed, and every page of a brochure with repeats whose loops changed (`repeatSignature`: its sections, template pages and language), or of any brochure whose language changed, are filled from their rows (data's `syncDataFor`); a brochure that has no repeats, had none before the edit and has no copies left is skipped. `repeatCopy` isn't data's `dataCopy`: data fills a generated page's layers like any other's, in the scope of its loops. Pasted, a generated page is an ordinary one (`prepareRepeatCopies`). **Scopes:** `pageScopeProvider` answers a template or generated page frame with its `ExpandedPage.layers` (expanding each brochure once per `root`, memoized on it), `pageLocaleProvider` any brochure page with its brochure's language. **Counting the rest:** `pages()` (`brochurePages(doc, record, expansion?)`, `brochureExpansion`) follows the expansion: template pages and made copies, a pending page (`pending`: its `ExpandedPage`, a made-up slot with the expanded id, its template's frame) for each copy that prints but isn't made, a template page whose list is empty for its row marked `notPrinted`. `bookLayout` gives pending pages a side and `notPrinted` ones none, so numbers, `{pages}` and the table of contents count every page; sections start on their first page that prints within their `first`/`end` range (`pageStarts`; a section with none, a repeating one whose list is empty, is in no `path`, so the contents leave it out, and it never reaches the back cover), copy sections with their own ids and names. Pending pages get no stamps (`stampTargets`), aren't arranged (`arrangedPages`), checked or selected (`selectedSlots`), and agents can't edit them (`requirePagePosition`); `printedPages` throws `Pending pages must be made before printing.` for one and leaves `notPrinted` ones out. `DesignBrochure`: `expansion()`, `pendingPages()` ("+N pages at export"), `setRepeat({ section } | { slot }, repeat | null)` (one undo step, `repeatEdit`: merged over the repeat there, a list required; `null` removes the frames and slots its loop made, `generatedLayers`, and leaves the template pages as normal pages).

**Repeats in the page list and panel** (`ui/`). A section folder's menu has "Repeat for each row…" and a template page's "Repeat this page…" ("Repeat settings…" once it repeats, and Remove repeat): they select the section's pages or show the page, whose panel sets it. `BrochureRepeatSection` (`ui/sidebar/`) shows for a template page (its own repeat and those of the sections the user started on it) and for a selection that is exactly a section's made pages (`selectedSection`, what a click on its folder selects): while it doesn't repeat, a button picking a list in scope (data's `listNames` over `useBrochureRepeat`'s scope: the sources, then the loops around it at their preview rows, `loopsAround`, read from its first template page in the expansion, layered with data's `layeredScope`); with a repeat, data's `RepeatRowsFields` (list, row name, key, filter, sort), "Rows per page" (pages only), `LimitFields` (Limit and "While designing, make", 3 unset), `PreviewRowInput` over every row, "N copies from M rows" and Remove repeat, each a `setRepeat` through data's `runRepeat` (a field emptied is taken out of the record). A page a repeat made shows `GeneratedPageSection` (it follows its template, which a button shows) instead of its master, sections and repeat. In the list (`pageTree`): a repeating section or page carries a repeat badge and every row of its run a bracket (`RepeatBracket` at the depth of the outermost repeating folder, `PageTreeRow.bracket`); copies carry the data mark (`RepeatMark`; a page reads "Copy of <template page>"), copy sections are folders that only fold, select and detach or delete their pages (no rename, drag, drop or section menu); next pending pages are one `PendingPagesRow` ("+36 pages at export", copy sections not made get no folder, a spread whose sides are all pending no heading), never selected, dragged or dropped on; a template page that doesn't print for its row (`notPrinted`, no side in the layout) is listed after the page before it, dimmed, "Not printed for this row". A folder closes where its section's pages end (`StartingSection.end`, the page number after its range in the expansion), so pages after a run (the back cover) are in no copy's folder or bracket; `selectedSection` walks the same way. `brochureExpansion` is remembered per brochure until its record, a source, the snapshots this device holds or the data phase change, so every row and panel shares one expansion per edit. Slot edits (adding, moving) count the stored slots, while the page list, Alt+↑/↓ and agents count the pages shown (pending and not printed ones too): `storedPosition` turns a place among the pages shown into one among the stored slots (before the first stored page at or after it, so a page dropped after a pending run lands after it; moving up, before the last stored page at or before it). Pending pages are never drop targets.

**Copies by hand** (Review Focus 4). A page a repeat made never moves: dropping one in the page list, Alt+↑/↓ on one and agents' `move_brochure_page` are refused ("Pages a repeat made follow their template…", a toast). One page at a time: taking one out of the brochure (×, the menu, Delete in the Catalog mode, the panel's Detach, `remove_brochure_pages`) detaches it instead, deleting one (Shift+Delete, deleting its frame anywhere: `pruneBrochure`, the `onRemove`) removes it, and one left out of `set_brochure_pages` or unticked with the pick tool is detached and taken out (`setSlots` keeps a page printing the frame a page printed as that page, `keepingSlots`). Each is `detachPages` (pure; `BrochurePageEdits.removePages`/`removeFrames`/`setSlots`/`pick` and `pruneBrochure`, one undo step): the page, named by the rows it shows (`ExpandedPage.rows`: the row of every loop around it, template passes included, `copyRows`; `expandedId(from, rows)`), joins its innermost loop's `detachedPages`, so `expandBrochure` makes no copy for it in either phase while the rest of its copy is still made (`previewRow` is only the user's own template's while designing: in an export, and for every pass inside a copy, a loop starts at its first row not detached, so a copy's page shows the same rows in both); a detached one keeps its slot as a plain page (`detached: { owner, from, path }`, no `generated`) that stands in the copy's place in the expansion, so it keeps its place among its copy's pages. A whole copy goes from its folder's menu (`CopyFolderMenu`) when the folder is a copy of its own repeat (`copyPassPath`; another folder inside a copy, a subsection or an inner repeat's template pass, detaches or deletes only its own pages, one by one): Detach (`detachSection`, `detachPasses`: the copy path joins its loop's `detached`, so the row's pass isn't walked, and every page made in it stays a plain page after the slot before it, `placeDetached`, which keeps it in only the sections whose `rows` it showed: never inside another copy's, after it instead) or Delete (`deleteCopySection`: detach the copy, then delete its frames and those of its pages detached before, one step). A copy taken out is all plain layers (`freedCopyPatches`, data's `freedPatch` on every layer but the master stamps: no `repeatCopy`, no `dataCopy`, no lock, no card repeater's `repeat`, no data fields), so it keeps what it shows. A `detached` slot is never a template (`templateSlots` leaves it out), starts no section and can't repeat (`repeatEdit` throws; the menu and panel hide those). What a loop that's gone leaves (its repeat removed, its section or page deleted) is cleared by `withoutStaleDetached` (in `repeatEdit`, the slot edits and `pruneBrochure`): `detached`/`detachedPages` keys through it, and the `detached` mark of a page no loop that still repeats holds (a plain template page again). `storedPosition` passes over copies too (their stored place is the sync's), so Alt+↑ next to a run moves above its template. `DesignBrochure` is split by concern: `BrochureHandle` (reads, `write`, arranging), `BrochurePageEdits` (page edits and detaching), `DesignBrochure` (repeats, settings, sections, masters).
