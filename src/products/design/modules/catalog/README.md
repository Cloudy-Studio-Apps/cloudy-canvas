# Catalog

Print brochures inside a design: in the Catalog mode a page's top-level frames are a brochure's pages, with spreads, bleed and margins, checks before printing and PDF export.

- **Tier:** feature
- **Requires:** core, pages, frame; uses: image
- **Contributes:** kinds `BrochureKind`; designCanvas: `editElement`, `hostCommands`, `kinds`, `modes`, `onRemove`, `tools`; designEditor: `leftTabs`, `modeSidebars`, `modes`, `overlays`, `sessionHooks`; `designMcpTools`, `designMigrations`
- **Points it owns:** —
- **MCP tools:** `add_brochure_pages`, `arrange_brochure`, `create_brochure`, `delete_brochure`, `duplicate_brochure`, `export_brochure_pdf`, `get_brochure`, `get_brochure_page`, `list_brochures`, `move_brochure_page`, `remove_brochure_pages`, `rename_brochure`, `set_brochure_page_options`, `set_brochure_pages`, `set_brochure_settings`, `view_brochure`
- **Files:** `brochure/` (the brochure records), `model/` (pages, spreads, arrange), `mode/` (the Catalog mode), `checks/` (DPI, placeholders), `export/` (PDF), `mcp/`, `ui/`.

## How it works

- **Catalog mode** (`catalog/`): a design page's top-level frames as a print brochure's pages, as a mode of the same file (one source of truth). A `brochure` record (`BrochureKind`, never on a page's canvas; `name`; `page`: the design page it belongs to, whose frames only it uses (`brochurePages` skips options on another page); a page holds any number; `openedAt`: the page's open brochure is the one opened last, else its first, written untracked; `slots`: `BrochureSlot { id, frames, chosen }`, a page's options, alternatives like "Opción A/B/C", and the one printed; `settings`: `layout` `book`/`single`, `covers` `both`/`front`/`back`/`none`, `splitSpreads`, `margin` mm, `dpi`; older records' `facing` becomes the layout in `fillDefaults`) is listed, made, renamed, duplicated, deleted and opened only through `DESIGN_BROCHURES` (`DesignBrochures`: `list(page)`, `current(page)`, `get`, `create(page, name?, open?)`, `rename`, `duplicate`, `remove`, `open`, one undo step each but `open`; `brochuresOf(doc)` where there's no engine; `currentBrochure(workspace)` is the open one of the workspace's page, `ensureBrochure` makes one when there's none, what picking, adding a blank page and the canvas's "+ Page" do) and edited through the `DesignBrochure` handle it gives (`pages()` resolves slots into `BrochurePage`s, skipping ids that are gone or no longer page frames, `isPageFrame`; `addPages`, `addOption`, `choose`, `move`, `removePages`, `removeFrames`, `pick`, `setSlots`, `setSettings`, `addNewPage`, one undo step each, over the pure `slot-edits.ts`); frames are kept by id, so renames are free, and `pruneBrochure` (an `onRemove`) takes deleted frames out of every brochure and deletes a deleted page's brochures in their deletion's undo step (duplicating a page doesn't copy its brochures yet). Files from before brochures had a page are split by the page of each slot's printed frame when they open (`splitLegacyBrochure`, a `designMigrations` entry). A world unit prints as a point (`paperSize`, `mmToUnits`; Paper presets are in points). `bookLayout(pages, settings)` (`DesignBrochure.layout()`) is how it prints and is read: `sides` (`BookSide`: a page, `part` `whole` or the `left`/`right` half of a spread frame, or a `blank` added so the back cover ends on the left or a spread starts on a left page), `spreads` (sides read together; in the book layout side `i` is a left page when `i + offset` is even, `offset` 1 with a front cover, alone on the right), `labels` ("6–7"), `spreadNumbers` and `pageCount`; a spread frame is one about twice the brochure's page (`brochurePageSize`, the commonest printed size, `spanOf`, `fitsPage`). The PDF prints `printedPages` (blanks as image-less pages, spread frames split in halves with `splitSpreads`, `drawFrame(…, part)`, `partRect`). `CATALOG_MODE`: `isolate: isPageFrame` (a click inside a frame reaches it), `faded: catalogFaded` (what the page's open brochure doesn't print, read through `ModeFadeContext.scope`; nothing while picking), `commands: CATALOG_ALLOWED_COMMANDS` (it organizes, never edits); its tool is `CatalogTool` (a `SelectTool` that doesn't move or resize; Delete takes pages out, the frames stay), with the hand and `CatalogPickTool` (ticks frames as the selection, starting with the printed ones; `applyPick` keeps ticked pages, drops unticked ones of the page on screen and adds new ones in `readingOrder`); `editCatalogPage` (double-click, Enter) designs a page. **Arranging on the canvas:** `settings.arrange` (`BrochureArrange`: `off`, the default, `below`, `beside`; `BROCHURE_ARRANGES`) lays the brochure's frames out (`model/arrange/`): `arrangeBlocks(layout, settings)` turns the layout's spreads into `ArrangeBlock`s (a page `left`/`right` of the spine or `across` it), `arrangeFrames(blocks, mode, origin)` places the printed pages on one line from the frames' current top-left (`below`: spreads left to right, each page's other options stacked under it; `beside`: a spread per row on one spine, a left page's options to the left, the others' to the right; lanes as wide as their widest frame, `ARRANGE_GAPS`), `pushAside(area, obstacles, gap)` moves the page's other top-level layers (each by its own and its contents' turned bounds) up or down out of the way, cascading onto what they'd land on, and `arrangeBrochure(doc, brochure, layout, pages, mode)` writes the moves (with descendants, no checkpoint) and returns each moved node's offset. While it isn't `off`, every `DesignBrochure` edit (choosing an option swaps it onto the line, adding, removing and moving pages, settings) arranges in the same undo step; `arrange()` (the Catalog toolbar's Arrange button, the sidebar's `ArrangeFields` Arrange now, agents' `arrange_brochure`) does it once, as `below` while it's off. Entering the mode, or showing another page or brochure in it, arranges again while it's on (`keepArranged`, the `useArrangeOnEnter` session hook: 150 ms after the switch shows, when the app is idle, so the mode appears at once and the frames then glide), as frames may have moved in the Design mode. Page thumbnails are `canvas-ui`'s `ElementThumbnail`s (kept while a frame only moved with all it holds, drawn when the app is idle). Moves are reported by `DesignBrochures.onArranged`, and `useArrangeGlide` (a session hook) has them glide with `engine.animateMoves`. `brochureIssues`/`checkBrochure` find blank pages the layout added and frames of another size (`layoutIssues`), hidden pages, image placeholders still to replace (`placeholder`, shown tagged in the page's Images with Replace, and as `placeholder: true` in `get_brochure_page`), pictures under `LOW_DPI` at print size (`effectiveDpi` by `scaleMode`, natural sizes from `pictureSize`, read once per asset) and text in the margin. `exportBrochure(workspace, brochure)` (and `checkBrochure`, `drawBrochureSheet`, which take the brochure too) draws each printed frame (`drawFrame`, on its design page through a workspace, one at a time) into `encodePdfPages`. UI (`catalog/ui/`; `useBrochure()` is the open brochure of the page on screen, `null` when it has none): the Brochure tab (`leftTabs` with `modes: ["catalog"]`: `BrochureSwitcher` atop it (the page's brochures, New brochure, Rename (also double-click), Duplicate, Delete), `NoBrochure` (Create brochure) on a page without one; rows drag to reorder, the selected page lists its options, grouped by the layout's spreads (Front cover, Pages 2–3, Back cover) with its blank pages as rows, `spreadLabel`, `BlankPageRow`; "+" adds a blank page like the last, the empty state picks frames or starts a blank A4 page, either way, or any frame size through frame's `FramePresetPicker`), the `modeSidebars` entry (`CatalogSidebar`: settings (`LayoutFields`: layout, covers, split spreads; the kit's `SelectField`/`CheckboxField`), checks, Export PDF and Preview; a page's options and pictures with Replace; a frame not in it; several frames; picking), `BrochureOverlay` (page badges, 9A/9B for options, "+ Page / + Option of page N" on the greyed frame under the pointer, margins, pick ticks and banner), `CatalogModeToolbar` (a `ModeToolbar` of items: its tools, the hand from its tool entry, the Pick/Preview/Export commands, Arrange), `BrochurePreview` (spreads, ← →, the strip; by default a 3D booklet, `BookSpread`, tilted in perspective with page edges and the spine's shade, whose pages turn over the spine, `TurningSheet` with the Web Animations API, laid out over the layout's sides by the pure `bookSlots`/`turningBook`/`restingBook`, a side being a page, half a spread frame (`PreviewPage` `part`, the whole picture clipped) or blank paper (`SidePicture`), the single layout centering each page by its width (`stageSize`, `sideWidth`) and timed by `usePageTurn`, skipped with reduced motion; the "3D view" switch, `catalog-view-store.preview3d`, shows them flat; pictures are drawn once and kept, `previewImagesOf`, and the spreads around are drawn ahead). Agents (`catalog/agent/`: `brochure-list`, `brochure-read`, `brochure-edit`, `brochure-settings` tool files over `brochure-args.ts` and `describe-brochure.ts`; pages by their 1-based `position`; every tool but `list_brochures` and `create_brochure` requires the `brochure` id, so agents never guess one, `readBrochure`): `list_brochures`, `create_brochure` (opened only when the page had none), `rename_brochure`, `duplicate_brochure`, `delete_brochure`, `get_brochure`, `get_brochure_page` (its pictures, `pagePictures`, with size and dpi), `view_brochure` (`drawBrochureSheet`: a spread, or a contact sheet of every spread with its numbers), `set_brochure_pages`, `add_brochure_pages` (frames, or a `blank` paper frame through `addNewPage`), `move_brochure_page`, `remove_brochure_pages`, `set_brochure_page_options` (`setOptions`), `set_brochure_settings`, `export_brochure_pdf`, each edit one undo step; the image module's `replace_image` swaps any layer's picture keeping its placement (`replaceImageFill`, shared with the page pictures' Replace). Not yet: bleed in the PDF, page numbers drawn on pages, master elements, a Catalog badge on Home's cards.
