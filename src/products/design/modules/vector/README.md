# Vectors

Vector networks: the pen (P) and pencil (Shift+P), and the edit mode for a vector's points with its own toolbar (`VectorEditToolbar`, a `ModeToolbar` of its five tools whose Done ends the edit).

- **Tier:** core
- **Requires:** core
- **Contributes:** kinds `VectorKind`; designCanvas: `editElement`, `kinds`, `pasteSvg`, `tools`; designEditor: `layerIcons`, `toolbars`, `tools`; `designMcpTools`
- **Points it owns:** —
- **MCP tools:** `vector_convert`, `vector_svg_import` (`mcp/import-svg.mcp.ts`: an SVG file as one vector, as dropping it does)
- **Files:** `svg/` (SVG files as one vector: `svgNetwork`, `svgToVector`, `svgPaint`; pasted and dropped SVGs through `designCanvas.pasteSvg`), `vector.kind.ts` (with `vector-area-bindings.ts`, `vector-area-paints.ts` and `vector-spec-network.ts`), `pencil.tool.ts`, `shape-to-vector.ts`, `edit/` (the edit mode), `vector.mcp.ts`, `ui/`.

## How it works

- **Vectors** (`vector/`): `VectorKind` (`network`; old pencil `points` become a chain in `fillDefaults`): regions filled, segments stroked, hit near a segment or inside a filled region, stretched when resized. `PencilTool` (Shift+P, a `FreehandTool`) draws chains; the pen and the pencil share the toolbar's `draw` menu. **Edit mode** (`vector/edit/`): `editVector` (the `editElement` hook) starts it on a vector, or first turns a shape with a `vectorNetwork` into a vector with the same id and look (one undo step). `VECTOR_EDIT` (`VectorEditSession`) holds the edited vector and the selected points, segments and areas (regions, as `RegionPick`s, so an undo that puts an area back before them keeps them on the same areas); picked areas are mirrored into the core's `REGION_SELECTION` (given at `start`, cleared at `stop`), so the Fill section edits their own fills. Every edit tool extends `VectorEditTool` (the overlay `drawVectorOverlay` as a guide, `partAt` for handles, points and segments, `write`/`commit` through `placeNetwork`, only Enter (Done) leaves, Esc lets go of the selected points and areas, V is Move points and other tool shortcuts wait, Delete removes parts (with only areas picked, it unfills them, `removeRegions`), selected areas are tinted in the overlay, Ctrl+A selects all points): `VectorMoveTool` (`vector-move`: pick, Shift, marquee, drag points, segments and handles, Alt breaks the mirror, a click inside an area picks it (`regionAt`, the topmost; Shift adds or removes), a click away lets go of the points), `PenTool` (`pen`, P: starts a vector outside the mode; adds points joined to the selected end, pulling handles while dragging; splits segments; joins points, closing loops into regions; Esc lets go of the end), `VectorBendTool` (curve a segment through the pointer, click a point to toggle smooth), `VectorBucketTool` (`vector-bucket`: click an enclosed area to fill or unfill it; a vector without fills gets the default gray) and `VectorCutTool`. `VectorEditToolbar` (Move, Pen, Bend, Paint bucket, Cut, Done = Enter) is contributed to `toolbars`.

An area's own image fills are in the kind's `assets`, make a flip draw mirrored and have their tiles scaled by the Scale tool, as a layer's do; the kind's `geometryKey` is its curves and filled loops, so a new area fill doesn't work the shape out again; the paint bucket fills an area emptied of its own fills (`fills: []`) instead of removing it.

**Bindings:** `VectorKind.bindable` (`areaBindable`) offers each area with fills of its own a color style (`network.regions.<i>.fills`; `bindPatch` writes it as the area's own fills with `setRegionFills`) and the colors of an area's own fills variables, as a layer's; data bindings take them and an area's pictures. When an edit adds or removes areas, their bindings move along (`areaBindingsPatch`, in the edit tools' `write`, the paint bucket and `completePatch` for agents; `regionIndexMap`: the same index while the count stays, else each area matched in order to the next with equal fills); data bindings on areas follow them too, and go when their area does or shows the layer's fills again (the data module's `followAreas`). The Fills section gives an area showing the layer's fills its own before applying a style to it, in the same undo step; areas keep ids (`withRegionIds`), so picks survive renumbering edits and undo. Page colors and gradients and the color wizard read areas' fills through `nodePaintStacks`.
