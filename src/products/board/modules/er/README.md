# ER diagrams

Chen-notation ER diagrams (extended model) on a board: drawn in the ER mode or written as text, checked against a standard and turned into database tables.

- **Tier:** feature
- **Requires:** —; uses: db, geo; after: section
- **Contributes:** kinds `ErAggregateKind`, `ErAttributeKind`, `ErConstraintKind`, `ErEntityKind`, `ErIsaKind`, `ErRelationKind`, `ErUnionKind`; boardCanvas: `editElement`, `hostCommands`, `kinds`, `links`, `modes`, `onRemove`, `prepareCopies`, `resolveElements`, `tools`, `withMembers`; boardEditor: `contextMenu`, `modes`, `overlays`, `sessionHooks`, `sidePanels`, `tools`; `boardErConstructs`, `boardErStandards`, `boardMcpTools`, `boardMigrations`
- **Points it owns:** `boardErStandards`, `boardErConstructs` (`er.points.ts`): the standards diagrams follow and what they may forbid; plugins add theirs
- **MCP tools:** `apply_er_text`, `check_er_diagram`, `convert_relation_to_entity`, `er_to_tables`, `get_er_text`, `list_er_diagrams`, `relayout_er`
- **Files:** `er.canvas.ts`, `er.mode.ts`, `er.commands.ts`, `er.sections.ts` (wiring), `entity/`, `relation/`, `attribute/`, `isa/`, `aggregate/`, `constraint/`, `union/`, `base/`, `model/` (`ErModel`, the ER language), `links/`, `connect/`, `ui/`.

## How it works

The module file (`er.module.ts`) only wires: `er.canvas.ts` (kinds, the tool, the lines and the hooks that
keep a diagram whole), `er.mode.ts` (the ER mode; the module adds `enterErMode` and the palette, which also shows outside the mode), `er.commands.ts` (the create tables and convert
commands and their ids), `er.sections.ts` (the toolbar button, the ER panel, menus and dialogs) and
`er.points.ts` (standards and constructs).

- `er/`: ER diagrams (Chen notation with the extended model; the language is documented in `docs/er-language.md`, with its sources), one family in one folder. **The ER roadmap is complete**: every construct of the theory (Chen and the extended model: weak entities, all attribute kinds, n-ary and recursive relations, join attributes, hierarchies, aggregation, constraints, associative entities, alternate keys, existence and identification marks, union types), the standards with their constructs, the Check diagram, the ER mode and ER to tables are covered, so `docs/er-roadmap.md` was removed. The only open point is confirming the approximate standards (`elmasri`, `silberschatz`, `piattini`: settings, `forbids`, `check`) and how the E mark is drawn against the books:
  - `base/`: `ErKind` (part of a diagram; created by the `er` tool or the ER text, never from an agent's spec; agents may only move them), `ErNamedKind` (the name is edited in place on one line; elements sized by their name resize round their center), `ER_DEFAULTS`, sizes (`er-size.ts`), outlines (`er-outline.ts`) and the shared types. Every element has `diagram` (an id) and `diagramName` (repeated on each element of the diagram): **a board holds several diagrams**.
  - One folder per type: `entity/` (rectangle, doubled when `weak`), `relation/` (diamond, `identifying`, `idMark`; `participants` point **by id** at entities or aggregations, with `(min,max)`, a role and `arrow`), `attribute/` (`ownerId` is an entity, relation, composite attribute, hierarchy or aggregation; key/multi/derived/optional flags, a `look`, an `order`), `isa/` (hierarchy triangle with `parentId` and children), `aggregate/` (a frame, hit by its name strip or border) and `constraint/` (hit near its line). Aggregations' and constraints' geometry is derived (`model/resolve-er.ts`, the board's `resolveElements`).
  - `model/`: `ErModel` (board-level facade: `diagram(id)`, `diagrams()`, `shape(id)`, `remove(id)`, `newDiagram(center?)`) and `ErDiagram` (`text()`, `applyText`, `textRange`, `relayout`, `rename`, `edits`). The text is parsed with the DBML tokenizer (`lang/`) and reconciled by name then position so ids and positions survive (`reconcile-er.ts`, `reconcile-extended.ts`); `applyErPlan` writes one undo step and lays out new elements (`layout/`, deterministic auto-layout). `withErMembers` is the board's `withMembers` (an element takes its attributes; dragging an aggregation takes what it holds), `pruneEr` part of its `onRemove`. Boards from before diagrams existed are split into diagrams when the file opens (`migrateErDiagrams`, the er module's `boardMigrations` entry, run by `openBoardDocument`).
  - `links/`: `ErLinks`, the lines (participants with cardinality labels, attributes, hierarchies; loops for self-relations).
  - **Building without the text:** `ErEdits`' adds take `at` (a center: `applyErPlan`'s `pinned` keeps the new shape there and places the rest round it), `addEntity` a `weak` and `addAttribute` a `key`. `erAddActions(edits, shapes, selected)` (`model/er-add-actions.ts`) is what a click adds per `ErPaletteItemId` (shared by the ER panel's Add menu and the palette); `erDropPlan(item, hit, diagram)` (`model/er-drop.ts`) what a drop does (`ErDrop`: a label, `run(edits, at)`; attributes need an owner, a hierarchy an entity, an aggregation a relation). `ui/er-palette/` (`ErPalette`, an overlay at the canvas's left edge: always in the ER mode, otherwise while an ER element is selected or the ER tool is on; with no diagram shown, an entity click or drop starts one, `erAddActionsWithoutDiagram`, `ErModel.newDiagram(center, { weak })`, and a drop's `run(er, at)` gets the whole `ErModel`): each type is clicked or dragged onto the board with pointer events (`usePaletteDrag`: `engine.clientToWorld`, `engine.elementAt`, a ghost chip with the drop's label; Esc cancels). Entities, relations and hierarchies show connect dots (`connect/`: `erConnectHandles`, `erConnectInteraction`); `ErConnectGesture` drags a line from one: entity → entity or aggregation makes a relation between them (at their midpoint), entity → relation or relation → entity/aggregation adds a participant, hierarchy ↔ entity makes the entity a child (not its parent, not twice), dropped on empty board it opens the quick-add menu there (`ER_QUICK_ADD`, an `ErQuickAdd` service holding the request; `ui/er-quick-add/` `ErQuickAddMenu`, an overlay following the point; choices from `erQuickAddOptions(source)`: an entity's attribute, key attribute, related entity (`ErEdits.addRelatedEntity`), relation or subtype (`addSubtype`); a relation's attribute or participating entity (`addParticipantEntity`); a hierarchy's child entity (`addIsaChildEntity`, also an Entity dropped from the palette on a hierarchy) or discriminator). Compound adds are one undo step and pin their extra shapes with `ErEditResult.pinned` (the link halfway). A dot dropped on another entity or aggregation opens the relation prompt instead (`ER_RELATION_PROMPT`, an `ErPrompt<ErRelationRequest>` like `ER_QUICK_ADD`; `ui/er-relation-prompt/`: `ErRatioPicker`, one of `ER_RELATION_RATIOS` (one to one, one to many, many to one, many to many: Chen participation cardinalities, the `joins` ends whose attribute the other links to) with `ErRelationGlyph`'s crow's-foot picture and a sentence; then `ErJoinStep` (`join-options.ts`: the key by default, a new key `id`, or none)), which makes it with `ErEdits.addLinkedRelation(ends, { at })` (`ErRelationEnd`: cardinality and `ErJoin`; through `ErModel.join` when the diagrams differ). **Join attributes:** `ErParticipant.via` is the attribute (by id) of its entity the relation joins through, written `Cliente.id (0,n)` (S25 in `docs/er-language.md`; `joinVias` in `reconcile-er.ts` resolves it after every owner's attributes are built, `addMissingEntities` adds missing ones on commit, `withoutDeadLinks` clears a deleted one, a changed `entityId` clears it); `ErLinks` labels it `(0,n) · id` (and a binary relation's lines end in crow's-foot marks read across from the other participant's `(min,max)`: `CrowFoot`, `links/crow-foot.ts`, a bar or fork next to the entity, then a circle or bar), the participant form has a "Joins by" select and the text completes attributes after `Entity.` (`entityAttributeNames`). **Standards and notation** (S27 in `docs/er-language.md`): `ErDiagramFields.notation` (an `ErNotation`: a `standard`, an `ErStandard`, and the `ErNotationSettings` it overrides: `cardinality` `minmax`/`ratio` (Chen's 1/N/M, `chenLetter`), `ratio` `across`/`own`, `participation` `numbers`/`lines`/`both`, `isa` `arc`/`circle`, `crowFeet`) is stamped on every shape like the name (`diagramScope`, `applyErPlan`; `ErDiagram.setNotation(patch)`, one undo step; merges keep the target's). Standards are a registry: each is an `ErStandardDefinition` written with `defineErStandard` (`base/standards/`, one `<id>.standard.ts` per built-in: `cloudy`, the default, `chen`, `elmasri`, `silberschatz`, `piattini`; its citation, `url`, `approximate`, `summary`, `settings`, `forbids` (construct ids it leaves out) and an optional `check(shapes)` of its own rules) contributed to the er module's `boardErStandards` point (`er.points.ts`) (the er module gives the built-ins, plugins theirs through `api.board.erStandards`; see `docs/plugins.md`), and read only through `erStandards` (`ErStandardRegistry`: `list`, `get`, `resolve`, pointed at the module host with `useSource`, the built-ins outside an editor), so nothing else names a standard. `ErStandard` is a `string`; an id that isn't installed is kept, drawn with the default and reported (a parse warning, a `standard-missing` finding, "(not installed)" in the picker). `erNotation(n)`/`erStandard(n)` resolve it; shapes saved without one load as `LEGACY_ER_NOTATION` (`ratio: own`) and a stored `preset` is dropped (`ErKind.fillDefaults`). The text reads and writes it on the `Diagram` line (`model/lang/notation-settings.ts`, `notation:` still read as `standard:`); `cardinalityRatio`, `ErLinks` (labels, `ErLink.double` lines, crow's feet), `drawErIsa` and `er-outline.ts` (the d/o circle) read it. **Constructs** (`base/constructs/`, S27): what a diagram can use that a standard may leave out, one `<id>.construct.ts` each, an `ErConstructDefinition` (`defineErConstruct`: `id`, `label`, `detect(shapes)` → the shape ids using it) contributed to the er module's `boardErConstructs` point (`er.points.ts`) (plugins through `api.board.erConstructs`) and read through `erConstructs` (`ErConstructRegistry`, `useSource` like the standards). A standard's `forbids` lists them (`erAllows`, `forbiddenConstructs`); `constructGate(standard)` (`gateOf(shapes)`/`standardOf(shapes)` in `model/standard-of.ts`, `useErGate()` in React) says what the editor may add, with the reason: `erAddActions`/`erDropPlan` disable or refuse what it forbids (`PALETTE_CONSTRUCTS`; `ErAddAction.blocked` greys the palette item), quick-add options carry a `construct`, `ErConnectGesture` won't link what makes one (`ErConnectOutcome.construct`), the relation prompt skips the join step without `join-attributes`, and form toggles can't be turned on (`lockedOn`; `FlagToggle.disabled`). What a diagram uses anyway is a `standard-construct` error per shape in the check, and `applyText` still applies it and returns it as warnings on the typed text's lines (`standardTextWarnings`). **Check diagram** (S28): `checkErDiagram(shapes, standard)` (`model/check/`, the shared rules then `standard.check`, `ErIssue`: `rule`, `severity`, `message`, `shapeId`), `ErDiagram.check()`, the `check_er_diagram` agent tool. The ER panel's Visual tab with no shape selected (`ui/er-panel/diagram-settings/`; while no diagram is shown, `ErDiagramList` lists the board's diagrams with their entity and finding counts, picking one fits it and shows its settings, plus New diagram; "All diagrams" (the settings' back link and the diagram switcher's menu, `useShowAllDiagrams`) goes back to it; `ErStandardPicker` with the reference's link through `openExternal`, `ErCheckList`, then `ErSettingOption` cards with `ErSettingPreview` pictures, from `ER_SETTING_CHOICES`) shows and sets all of it; the ER mode's Check button opens it with the count of errors and warnings. **ER mode** (`mode/`, `ui/er-mode/`): `erMode` (`er.mode.ts`, one `defineBoardMode` object registered with `registerBoardMode`; `ER_MODE_ID`: the select tool with the hand and ER tools, isolating `isErElement`, every diagram, so linking across still joins them) and its switcher entry ("ER diagrams") and toolbar (`useErToolbarItems`, `run` items drawn by `ModeToolbar`, titled with the mode's name: New diagram at the view's center, Select diagram, Re-layout, Diagram text, Check, Done = `useEditorMode().leave`). It's entered from the mode switcher, by `enterErMode` (the er module's `editElement`: double-click or Enter on an ER shape; inside the mode, double-click renames), by the ER tool after placing a new diagram and by the panel's Edit diagram button; the palette and ER panel stay open while it's on (the panel's × folds it to a bar, `ErPanelCollapsed`, `erCollapsed` in `board-panel-store`; the toolbar's Diagram text and Check unfold it; leaving the mode closes it). **More of the model:** associative entities (`ErRelationElement.associative`, `[associative]`, S29: a diamond in a rectangle, a participant like an entity, `isErParticipant`, its own table) and `convertRelationToEntity` (`model/edits/convert-relation.ts`, `ErEdits.convertToEntity`, the `er.convertToEntity` host command in the context menu, `canConvert`, the relation form, the `convert_relation_to_entity` agent tool); alternate keys (`ErAttributeKey` `alternate`, `[ak]`, S30); the existence mark (`existenceMark`, `[existence]`, S31; the `dependency` setting hides both marks); union types (`union/`: `ErUnionKind`, `categoryId`, `superclassIds`, `Union Category { … }`, S32: parse, serialize, reconcile, layout `place-unions.ts`, links with the ⊂ mark, a form, connect dots, a palette item); Silberschatz's `arrows: cardinality` and `isa: arrow` settings; `joins: shown | hidden` (the join attribute after the cardinality, shown by `cloudy` only). Line labels are centered from their estimated size (`labelPoint`, `LINK_LABEL_FONT_SIZE`), clear of the crow's feet and the line. **Layout** (`model/layout/`): the entity grid starts at a hierarchy's root and places close partners (a weak entity and its owner, an aggregation's members, `closePartners`) next to each other; diamonds step aside from the midpoint until they and their lines clear other shapes and foreign aggregation boxes (the fewest conflicts otherwise, `place-relations.ts`), and `clearForeignDiamonds` moves any left on a box out; attributes stay near their owner (every direction at a distance before going further, the least-conflict spot as a fallback, `fan-out.ts`), discriminators are placed first, and an aggregation's own attributes go along the outside of its real box (`placeAggregateAttributes`). **ER to tables** (S33): `erToTables(shapes, { hierarchies, existingType })` (`model/to-tables/`: `entityTables` (hierarchies per entity or single, weak entities after their owners), `unionTables`, `relationTables` (foreign keys or link tables, aggregations and associative entities first), `multivaluedTables`, `writeDbml`; column types guessed by name, `guessColumnType`), `ErDiagram.tablesPreview`/`createTables` (`importSchemaText`, merging by name, keeping edited column types), the `ErToTablesDialog` (`ui/er-to-tables/`: a hierarchy choice each, the preview, the errors and notes) opened from the ER mode's toolbar, the diagram settings and the `er.createTables` context menu command, and the `er_to_tables` agent tool. The ER mode's keys are `ER_MODE_COMMANDS` (N, A, Shift+A, R, I; `erClickActions`, shared with the palette; the commands' `run` is `runErModeCommand`), and `ErCheckBadges` (an overlay of the mode, mounted only while it's on) draws its findings on the shapes; a selected shape's own findings (and its attributes') are also listed above its form in the panel, in or out of the mode (`ShapeIssues`, `ui/er-panel/forms/shape-issues.tsx`). Form edits that unlink shapes split the diagram (`ErEdits.run`'s `unlinks`, `applyErPlan`'s `after`). **Joining and splitting diagrams:** linking a shape of another diagram (a connect dot dropped on it) runs the edit through `ErModel.join(into, from, edit)`, which first moves `from`'s shapes into `into` (`mergeErDiagrams`: names `into` already uses get a free one, `name_2`), one undo step; palette drops attach in the diagram of what they're dropped on (`ErDrop.diagram`). Deleting on the canvas (the er module's `onRemove`, after `pruneEr`) or with `ErModel.remove` runs `splitErDiagrams(doc, before)`: each part of a diagram the deletion cut off from what it was linked to gets a new diagram (the largest part keeps the id and name); shapes that were already unlinked stay, and edits of the ER text never split.
