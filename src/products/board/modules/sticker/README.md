# Sticker (Board module)

_How it works. The rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../../../CLAUDE.md); names may still follow the pre-refactor conventions until their phase (see its Transition table)._

## Sticker

- `sticker/`: `StickerKind extends BoxKind`, a stamped reaction (`keepsRatio`, not connectable, not in `create_shapes`: agents use `list_stickers` and `stamp_stickers`, `sticker.mcp.ts`, which take built-in, plugin and emoji ids but not custom ones, `findStampableSticker`). Its picture is a document asset whose id derives from the sticker's catalog id (`stickerAssetId`: `sticker:seal:happy`, `sticker:emoji:1f44d`, `sticker:custom:<id>`; a recolored edge adds `~edge=<hex>`), so every stamp of one sticker shares one copy; `label` and `sticker` feed `describe`. The `sticker` tool (K) stamps the `sticker` tool option (`PickedSticker`: the catalog id, label, asset id and the picture's bytes, stored with `doc.addAsset` on first use) and stays on, with a preview under the pointer; each stamp pops in with `ctx.animateIn`. **Stickers are shared by both products** (the design's are in its `sticker/` module): the catalog is `shared/core/stickers/` (`catalog/`: the "foca watona" moods (`seal/`, SVG built from a shared head plus eyes, mouths and props) and the common stamps (`common/`), both wrapped by `stickerSvg(art, defs, look?)` (a die-cut edge, white or `StickerLook.edgeColor`; a `StickerDef.restyle(look)` draws it again), and the emojis (`emoji/`: Twemoji art from `@twemoji/svg` via `import.meta.glob`, names from `emojibase-data`, groups static in `emoji-groups.ts`; loaded with a dynamic `import()`)), held by the app-level registry `stickerCatalog` (`StickerRegistry`: `registerGroup`, `registerSet`, `sections(tab)`, `get`, `find`, `loadEmojis`, `drop(owner)`, `subscribe`; plugins fill it through `api.stickers`), with `stickerPicture`/`pickSticker` (one cache of pictures for both editors) and `stickerSourceOf` (a sticker of either product, for pasting across). The picker is `canvas-ui/stickers/` (`StickerWheel({ activeId, onPicked })`: a wheel of the recently used stickers (`shared/stores/sticker-recents-store`, persisted) around a button that opens a tabbed panel (Stickers with the plugins' groups, Emojis, Custom) with one search); each product's module shows it through a wrapper (`BoardStickerWheel`, `DesignStickerWheel`). Custom stickers are the user's library (`StickerLibrary` in `shared/core`, `stickerLibrary` and `importStickerFiles` in `@platform`: files in `<appData>/stickers/` with an `index.json`), held by `shared/stores/custom-stickers-store`. A sticker copied in one product pastes as the other's (`pasteForeignSticker`, the `pasteForeign` hook).
