# Workspaces

_How it works. The rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md)._

## Workspaces

- **Workspaces** (`canvas/workspace/`): a collaborator's own context on the document, so the user's page, mode, tool, selection and undo never change what it works on. `CanvasWorkspace<O>` is the context-level API agent tools use (elements of its scope, scope, selection, `userSelection()`, guides, the edits, exports, `zoomToFit`/`zoomToElement`, undo); `CanvasEngine` implements it for the user and `engine.workspace(id, init?)` makes an `ActorWorkspace` for a collaborator (`findWorkspace`, `dropWorkspace`; all dropped on close); `engine.scratchWorkspace(fork, init?)` makes one over a fork of the document (`CanvasDocument.fork`, see [document/](../document/README.md#forks)), with the product's `afterEdit` set on the fork, never listed with collaborators', dropped by its caller (`dropScratchWorkspace`) or when the engine is destroyed; its exports read the fork's pictures (`readAsset`). `workspace.exportCopy()` (on the engine and on any `ActorWorkspace`, through `ActorWorkspaceHost.exportCopy`) is the way to get one: a `ScratchCopy { workspace, doc, dispose }` on the workspace's page, the fork readied by the product in one transaction of the scratch workspace's document (`CanvasProduct.exportCopy.prepare`, composed from the `exportCopies` point: each hook that `needed` the copy prepares it; its writes run `afterEdit` as usual; when those passes run out, `doc.settled()`, it throws and disposes the fork); `dispose()` drops the workspace and destroys the fork, once. `workspace.needsExportCopy()` is the product's `exportCopy.needed` on the document, and `withExportCopy(workspace, work)` runs an export on the workspace or, when needed, on a copy disposed however it ends (the export dialog, a design's layer exports and agents' exports go through it: a design's repeats past their design caps draw every row there) and its export host's parts are the fork's: a headless `Scene` of its scope (`productSceneHooks`, refreshed lazily), its selection, its `viewport` (`actor-viewport.ts`: `showArea`, `followEdit`, in the user's view proportions), its services (`ServiceCache` on its document) and its history. The edits the engine and workspaces share are plain functions over `WorkspaceParts` (`workspace/operations/`: delete, reorder, lock, move, arrange, transform, style, bounds, `sceneExportHost`). An export of a workspace's scene that isn't the canvas's draws with that scene's stacking, clips, masks and effects (`Scene.placement()`, handed to `Renderer.snapshot` as `placement`), since its elements may not be on the canvas.
