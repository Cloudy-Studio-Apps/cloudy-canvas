# Effects

_How it works. The rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md)._

## Effects

- **Effects** (`canvas/effects/` data, `render/effects/` drawing): a `CanvasEffect` (`id`, `type`, `visible`, plus its type's fields) is plain JSON; the product's `effectRenderers` (point `effectRenderers`, keyed by type) draw them on the GPU and its `effects(elements)` hook (one module) returns `ElementEffects`: an `EffectScope` per element with effects (`effects`, `home`, `box`, `rotation`, `bounds` of what it holds) and `homes` (element → its `EffectSlot`). Slots are a `ScopeSlot` (`MaskSlot | EffectSlot`), so masks' and effects' scopes nest in each other (`homeOf`, `scopeHome`, `sameSlot` in `layer-placement.ts`; a product gives both consistently, the design through `treeSlots`); `Scene.effects`, `Scene.visualBounds(el)` (shown bounds grown by `effectsOutset`, what exports frame) and `masksOf` walk them. `ElementLayer` puts an element's view and what it holds into an `EffectScopeView` (`render/layers/effect-scope-view.ts`: at the element's order key, clipped like its view, culled with its reach) whose one `EffectStackFilter` (a Pixi `Filter` overriding `apply`) runs the effects' `EffectRenderer`s stage by stage (`backdrop`, `content`, `behind`, `filter`) over the content texture, each `render(pass, effect, current)` returning the new result. An `EffectPass` gives `content`, `backdrop` (the back texture, for renderers with `backdrop`/`readsBackdrop`; the canvas then keeps Pixi's back buffer, `RendererOptions.backdrop`), `px`/`offset` (world → CSS px, turned with the element), `toLocal` (texture coordinates → the element's own), `texture()`, `run(shader, input)` and a Gaussian `blur(input, radius)` (`blurShader`, taps spread past `MAX_BLUR_TAPS`); textures are pooled and returned after. `EffectShader` wraps a GLSL ES 3 fragment (highp; `FILTER_HEADER`, `SAMPLING_GLSL`: `sampleIn`, `px`) with its uniforms (`set`) and samplers (`bind`); shared ones: `copyShader`, `silhouetteShader` (offset, round spread, invert, color), `underShader`, `atopShader`, `shapeUnderShader`. `ElementLayer.setEffectScale` is the zoom (1 in snapshots); on the canvas renderer effects are skipped. **Compositing:** an `EffectScope` may carry a `composite` (`ScopeComposite`: `opacity`, a `BlendMode` from `canvas/effects/` (`BLEND_MODES`, Figma's eighteen), `isolated`): the filter's last step is `compositeShader` (`render/effects/composite-shader.ts`, the W3C formulas over `pass.backdrop`, so the canvas keeps the back buffer for products with an `effects` hook), and a non-isolated opacity-only scope with no effects runs no filter, its container's `alpha` handing the opacity down. Paint layers take a `blendMode` too (`pixiBlendMode`: Pixi's own modes, `pixi.js/advanced-blend-modes` registered by the renderer; hue draws normal).
