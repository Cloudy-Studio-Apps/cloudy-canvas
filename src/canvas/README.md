# Canvas engine

_How it works. The rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md); names may still follow the pre-refactor conventions until their phase (see its Transition table)._

The engine is the same for every product. **A product is a `CanvasProduct`** (`canvas-product.types.ts`): its `kinds` (`ElementKinds`), `createTools(ctx)` (added after the built-in select and hand tools), `keybindings` and `defaultOptions`. `O` is the product's tool options type, threaded through `CanvasEngine<O>`, `ElementKind<E, O>`, `ToolContext<O>` and `EngineState<O>`. Products are assembled from modules with `composeProduct` (see Modules): the board's is `getBoardProduct()` (`products/board/ui/modules/board-modules.ts`), the design's `getDesignProduct()` (`products/design/ui/modules/design-modules.ts`).

## CanvasEngine

- `CanvasEngine` (`canvas-engine.ts`) is a coordinator, created with `{ product, theme, document? }`. It wires the pieces together:
  - `CanvasDocument`: the content, a Yjs doc.
  - `Camera`
  - `Scene`: element list, selection, hover and marquee, plus spatial queries.
  - `Renderer`: PixiJS.
  - The tools.
  - Input modules: pointer, wheel and keyboard.

It exposes `kinds`, `keymap`, `commands` and `elementInfo(type)` to hosts. `mount(container, onLoading?)` opens the canvas fitted to its content (at most 100%): it builds the views a slice at a time (`Renderer.syncElementsChunked`, about 12 ms per frame), then waits for the scope's images (`Renderer.preloadImages`) and fonts (`FontTracker.settled`), at most 8 s, reporting each step as an `EngineLoading`; input starts once it resolves. `useCanvasEngine` returns `{ engine, loading }` and both editors show `CanvasLoadingOverlay` (`canvas-ui`) until the engine is there. Hosts close it with `engine.close()` (`useCanvasEngine` does on unmount): input stops, the tasks set with `engine.onClose(key, task)` run while it can still draw (at most 3 s), then it's destroyed. `engine.clientToWorld(client)` (`null` outside the canvas) and `engine.elementAt(world)` let DOM drags drop onto the canvas. Pictures for the UI are encoded off the UI thread by `encodeImage(canvas, type, quality)` (`export/encode-worker/`: one module worker drawing an `ImageBitmap` into an `OffscreenCanvas`, `canvasBlob` where there's none or it fails). `engine.captureArea(rect, options)` is a JPEG of an area as the scope on screen shows it. `engine.thumbnail({ width, height })` is a small JPEG of the whole canvas framed to that size (`export/thumbnail.ts`), or `null` when it's empty: drawn from the views already built (`Renderer.captureView`: the world at identity, unculled, extracted; about a frame, no new views or image loads), never through `snapshot`, so large files stay cheap.

## Registries are per product, never global

- **Registries are per product, never global.** Everything that needs an element's kind (document, scene, renderer, overlay, text editor, tools via `ctx.kinds`, agent helpers) gets the product's `ElementKinds` and calls `kinds.kindOf(el)`.

## Data flow is one-way

- **Data flow is one-way.** Tools write to the document, and `doc.observe` reports `{ updated, removed, fields }` (`fields`: what each element changed, `"all"` when added; `changesOnly(change, GEOMETRY_FIELDS)` tells a move or resize apart, which feeds like the design's layers and pages ignore). The engine then refreshes the `Scene`, syncs only the changed Pixi views (`render/layers/element-layer.ts`) and requests a frame (fonts are asked for only for the changed elements).
  - A view only draws again when its element changed more than its `x`/`y` (`viewChange`, `render/view-change.ts`: fields by identity, then `deepEqual`); a moved one is only moved, so kinds must draw in local coordinates from the element's fields. What else a drawing depends on is `kind.drawnFrom(el)` (compared by identity; the design's boolean gives its result), and `renderer.syncElements(…, redraw)` / `engine.refreshElements(ids)` draw again anyway (a text's glyph outlines or font arrived).
  - Rendering is on demand: Pixi uses `autoStart: false`, and `FrameScheduler` renders at most once per animation frame.
  - **Render cost:** each frame, views outside `camera.visibleWorldRect()` are culled (`ElementView.cull`, from its cached drawn `extent()`). A clipped view gets a mask only when the clip cuts something off (`clipCovers`). Text textures are capped by their element's size (`cappedTextResolution`, at most 4096 px a side; resolution at most 4), and image textures are sized to the screen (`ImageCache`, `render/image-cache.ts`): each picture loads at a base level (`BASE_LEVEL`, 1024 px on its long side, `image-levels.ts`), every frame the views on screen say how sharp they need it (`ElementView.imageUses`, `imageDemands` in `image-demand.ts`: the picture's on-screen size from its placement and natural size, times zoom and resolution; `ImageCache.want`), and once that settles (150 ms) sharper levels (powers of two up to 4096, capped at the picture's own pixels) are decoded, two at a time, and the views showing it drawn again (`ElementLayer.redrawImages`, `ElementView.redraw`); zoomed out again, a picture on screen goes back down the same way (to the base at once, or a smaller level decoded anew; `settleLevels`); past a 768 MB budget the sharp levels of pictures out of view are let go (base levels never). Natural sizes come from file headers without decoding (`imageHeaderSize`, `canvas/image/`: PNG, JPEG with its EXIF turn, GIF, WebP; `knownImageSize`, `decodeImage` in `render/decode-image.ts`). **Exports don't use the canvas's textures:** `drawSnapshot` builds its views, asks them what they need at its scale and gets each picture at that size from an `ExportImages` (`render/export-images.ts`: a canvas level when one is sharp enough, else decoded from the file up to its own pixels and `maxTexturePixels`, the GPU's limit capped at 8192), draws them again and frees it after. `engine.imageQuality(assetId)` (`ImageQuality`: natural size, the copy shown, how large it shows now; `onImagesChange`) reports it, and `ImageQualityNote` (`canvas-ui`, `useImageQuality`; under each picture in the design's Fills and Strokes sections, `PaintListSection`, and in its `ImageEditor`) shows it with a tooltip saying exports and Catalog mode use the original.

## Product hooks

- **Product hooks** keep product features out of the engine:
  - `createLinks()` returns `CanvasLinks` (`links/`): lines derived from the elements (e.g. database relations), re-resolved on every document change and drawn under the elements, highlighted when they touch the selection.
  - Links can show text with `view.label(text, at, options)` (pooled text nodes, e.g. ER cardinalities).
  - **Element actions and DOM overlays:** a kind's `interactionAt` press can call `ctx.activate(elementId, action)` for something only the host can do (play a video, open a link); hosts listen with `engine.onElementAction`. DOM placed over an element follows it with `engine.worldRectToScreen(rect)` and `engine.onViewChange(listener)` (camera, resize and document changes).
  - `resolveElements(elements)` gives elements whose geometry follows others (ER aggregation boxes, constraints) their current geometry: `Scene.refresh()` runs it and keeps last refresh's object for an element that resolved the same (`deepEqual`, same `drawnFrom`), so only those that resolved differently (`Scene.derivedIds`) are synced. **Read geometry from the scene** (`engine.getElement`, `ctx.getElement`), not from `doc.get`; the stored bounds of derived elements are stale. The text editor places itself from the scene too.
  - `withMembers(ids, elements, purpose)` adds what goes with the ids when they're moved (`ctx.withMembers` in the select tool) or deleted (`engine.deleteElements`), e.g. an entity's attributes.
  - `onRemove(doc, removed, before)` cleans up after deletions (e.g. clears refs to deleted tables, frees connector ends), inside the same undo step. `before` is the scene as it was, so resolved geometry of removed elements is still readable.
  - `prepareCopies(copies, { copied, existing })` fixes copied elements before they're added (the board: free DB names via `prepareDbCopies`, a new ER diagram per source diagram via `prepareErCopies`, loose connector ends via `prepareConnectorCopies`). `pasteText(text, at, index, options)` turns text pasted from outside the app into an element (the board: a text element), and `pasteImage(image, view, index, options)` an image (the board: an image element, `createPastedImage`).

## Scopes

- **Scopes** (`CanvasScopes`, the product's `scopes`, one module at most): a document split into parts shown one at a time (the design's pages). `list(doc)` is the scope ids in order, `of(el)` an element's scope (`null`: never shown, e.g. the page records), `place(el, scope)` puts a new element in one and `label(doc, scope)` names it for people (`engine.scopeLabel`, the design's page names) and `background(doc, scope)` marks scopes the canvas never shows on its own (it doesn't open on them, undo and redo don't follow edits there, and they aren't shown when the scope on screen goes; only `setScope` does). The `Scene` holds only the current scope's elements (`Scene.scope`, `scopeIds`), so hits, selection, exports, thumbnails, links and agents' `engine.elements()` stay in it; `doc.setPlacement` (set by the engine) places every added element in the scope on screen, so tools, pastes and agents need no code for it. `engine.scopeOf(el)` is the scope an element is shown in (`CanvasScopes.of`), and `engine.rootOf(id)` the top-level element it's drawn in (`CanvasProduct.rootOf`, read from the document so any scope works: a design layer's top frame; without it every element is its own root). `engine.scope`/`engine.setScope(id)` (and `EngineState.scope`): switching clears the selection, goes back to the select tool and restores that scope's camera (or fits its content). When the scope on screen is deleted the next one shows; an undo or redo that only touched another scope shows that one. Elements updated into another scope leave the canvas like removed ones. `doc.addUntracked` adds outside the undo history (what a file needs when it opens).

## View, order and drops

- **View, order and drops:** `view` (`CanvasViewOptions`: `minZoom`, `maxZoom`, `grid`: `"dots"`, `"pixels"`, a one-unit grid drawn over the content from 800%, or `"none"`; unset values are `DEFAULT_VIEW_OPTIONS`, the board's) sets the camera's limits and the grid layer (`render/layers/create-grid-layer.ts`). `orderKeys(elements)` replaces `zOrderKey` as the drawing order (the design's layer tree): `Scene.orderKey` is what `ElementLayer` stacks by, and `Scene.restackedIds` are redrawn when a key changes without its element (a parent moved in the order). `clips(elements)` gives the world area clipped elements show in (`ElementClip`: a rect and a corner radius): `Scene.clipOf` is what `ElementLayer` masks views with (`ElementView.setClip`; layers get `LayerPlacement`: `orderOf` and `clipOf`), `Scene.elementAt` ignores clicks on clipped-away parts, and `Scene.replacedIds` (order or clip changed) are placed again. `editElement(el, ctx)` (point `editElement`; several modules may give one, the first returning `true` wins) edits an element its own way on double-click (after groups are entered) and Enter, before its text (the design's vector edit mode, the board's image crop and ER mode). Tools may have `onActivate()` (feedback that stays while they're on) and `onCommand(command)`, which every command asks first (`engine.toolTakes`), so a mode can own Esc, Enter, Delete and Ctrl+A; `ctx.getTool()` is the active one. The editor point `toolbars` (`ToolbarModeEntry`: `tools`, `component`) replaces the main toolbar while one of its tools is on (`ModuleToolbar`); the editor point `modes` (`EditorModeEntry`: the mode's `id`, `label`, `description`, `icon`, `order` and an optional `toolbar` shown instead of the main one while it's on) feeds the `ModeSwitcher` beside the toolbar (shown with two modes or more; the product's core module gives the default mode's entry, the board's "Board"), and `useEditorMode()` (`EditorModeContext`: `id`, `entry`, `isDefault`, `setMode`, `leave`) is what a mode's UI reads. `selectTarget(hitId, { selection, deep, enter }, elements)` decides what a click selects (the design's core reads `designSelectRedirects` first: a module's answer, e.g. a generated layer's sheet) (`ctx.targetAt`, `ctx.targetOf`; the select tool, double-click and right-click use it; e.g. a design group). `FreehandTool` (`tools/freehand-tool.ts`) is the base of freehand drawing tools (the board's marker, the design's pencil): it collects, previews, smooths (`smoothPoints`, `smooth-points.ts`) and adds. `onMoved(doc, moved, elements)` runs when the select tool finishes a drag (`ctx.moved`), in the drag's undo step (the design drops nodes into frames and reorders auto layouts). The canvas theme is read from the editor's container (`readCanvasTheme(container)`), so a product can override the `--canvas-*` tokens (`[data-product="design"]` in `tokens.css`).

## Undrawn elements

- **Undrawn elements:** the product's `undrawn(elements)` hook (one module) names elements that are hit, selected and moved but not drawn on their own (a design boolean's operands, which it draws combined): `Scene.drawn(el)`, `LayerPlacement.drawn`, `ElementView.setDrawn` (a view with an empty clip, what a hidden parent holds, isn't drawn either); snapping skips them. `engine.refreshElements(ids)` reads elements again with what derives from them though the document didn't change (a text's glyph outlines arrived).

## Frames

- **Frames:** a kind with `frame = true` (ER aggregation boxes, sections) is drawn in its own layer behind the links and the other elements, larger frames further back (`zOrderKey`, `elements/z-order.ts`), and a marquee selects it only when it encloses it.

## Feeds

- **Feeds:** board-level facades (`DbSchema`, `ErModel`) notify React through a `DocumentFeed` (`document/document-feed.ts`), which observes the document only while someone subscribes, so they need no cleanup and can be created in a `useMemo`.
  - Kinds offer `interactionAt` and `handles` (see Elements), which the select tool and overlay use.

## Context menu, z-order, lock and export

- **Context menu, z-order, lock and export:** a right-click selects what's under the pointer (locked elements too; empty space clears the selection) and reports a `ContextMenuRequest` to `engine.onContextMenu` listeners; `CanvasContextMenu` (`canvas-ui/components/context-menu/`) shows it, its items are data (`SELECTION_MENU`, `CANVAS_MENU`: engine commands, labels from `BASE_COMMAND_LABELS`; a `ContextMenuCategory` is a submenu of commands, like Copy/Paste as and Order, and a module's `contextMenu` group with a `label` becomes one, like the design's Transform, Mask and Boolean, so the menu stays short; menus past the window's height scroll, `MENU_FITS_SCREEN`). `reorderSelection` (`bringToFront` `]`, `bringForward` Ctrl+], `sendBackward` Ctrl+[, `sendToBack` `[`) rewrites indexes with `reorderIndexes` (`elements/reorder.ts`: front/back of everything, or one step past the nearest overlapping element; frames keep their area order). An element with `locked` is skipped by `Scene.elementAt` (unless asked), `elementsInRect`, `findInteraction`, `selectAll` and the select tool's drags (`toggleLock` Ctrl+Shift+L, `unlockAll` Ctrl+Alt+Shift+L). `pasteToReplace` (Ctrl+Shift+R) pastes centered on the selection and removes it in the same undo step (`ElementClipboard.paste({ replace })`). Exports (`canvas/export/`): `engine.exportElements({ format, background, area })` draws the selection (with its `withMembers(…, "move")`) or the whole canvas off screen (`Renderer.snapshot`, fresh layers and the product's `createLinks()`, images loaded first) as PNG or JPG (`ExportOptions.rect`: exactly that world area, no padding, e.g. a page frame), a one-page PDF (`encodePdf`, a hand-written JPEG page; `encodePdfPages(pages, { pointsPerUnit })` writes several, the Catalog's brochure with a unit as a point) or CSV of the elements whose kind has `rows(el)` (tables, DB tables; `encodeCsv`). `copyAsPng` (Ctrl+Shift+C) writes one with `Clipboard.writeImage`. The `export` command (Ctrl+Shift+E) is a host action: `ExportDialog` (`canvas-ui/components/export-dialog/`) handles it and saves with `exportFile` from `@platform`.

## Nudge, resize and size label

- **Nudge, resize and size label:** the arrow keys nudge the selection (`nudgeLeft`…, Shift: `nudgeLeftFar`…, 10 units; `engine.nudgeSelection`, `engine.moveElements` with `withMembers(…, "move")`, locked ones stay, no `onMoved`, so nothing changes parent); a burst of presses is one undo step (`nudgeCheckpoint`, `canvas/arrange/nudge.ts`; tools take them with `onCommand` and `nudgeStep(command)`, e.g. vector edit mode nudges its points). The resize handles belong to a `ResizeTarget` (`handles/resize-target.ts`, `resizeTarget(selected, kinds)`): one resizable element, or a multi-selection with a resizable element in it, by the box round the outlined ones; `ResizeGesture` scales each element into the new box (`scaleRectInBox`, `geometry.ts`) through `kind.resize` (ones that can't resize move by their center, with their members). Shift (or any `keepsRatio` kind) keeps the proportions, Alt resizes round the center (`resizeRect(…, { keepRatio, fromCenter })`). `CanvasViewOptions.sizeLabel` (the design's) shows the selection's `W × H` in a tag under it (`render/layers/size-label-layer.ts`, from `OverlayState.size`).

## Scale tool

- **Scale tool (K)** (Figma's): `ScaleTool` (`tools/scale-tool.ts`, a `SelectTool` with `resizeMode: "scale"`) is built in next to select and hand; products bind its key (`tool.scale`: K in the design, Shift+K on the board, where K is the sticker tool) and put it in the toolbar's `move` menu with select. The active tool's `Tool.resizeMode` is `Scene.resizeMode`, which `resizeTarget(selected, kinds, mode)`, `findInteraction` and the overlay read: in `scale` mode what's `scalable` gets handles (a line, a group, a board text too) and the box takes lines along. `ResizeGesture` in `scale` mode keeps the proportions and scales every element with its `withMembers(…, "move")` round the drag's fixed point (`resizeAnchor`: the opposite corner or side, the center with Alt; turned for a turned element) through `kind.scale(el, t)` (`ScaleTransform { origin, factor }`, `elements/element-scale.ts`: `scalePoint`, `scaleRect`, `scaleLength`, `scaleOffsets`; `scalePatches`, `canvas/arrange/scale.ts`). The default resizes a resizable element to its scaled box and moves the rest; `BoxKind` and `LineKind` (points) are `scalable`, and kinds scale what they draw: the design's nodes their `strokeWeight`, rectangles and frames their `cornerRadius`, texts `fontSize` and `letterSpacing` (laid out again), lines their ends, vectors their network (groups leave it to their children); the board's texts their `fontSize`, stickies and tables a `textScale` (font and insets, `tableTypography`), shapes their `fontSize` and `strokeWidth`, strokes and connectors their `size` (free connector ends move), images their `padding`; DB and ER elements only move. `engine.scaleElements(ids, factor, anchor)` (a `ScaleAnchor` of the box round them, `anchorPoint`, `SCALE_ANCHORS`) is one undo step: the design's `ScaleSidebar` (the tool's `toolSidebars` entry: W, H, %, the 3×3 `AnchorPicker` from `canvas-ui`) and the agents' `scale_shapes`/`scale_nodes` (`canvas/mcp/scale.mcp.ts`, part of `arrangeTools`: `factor`, `width` or `height`, `anchor`) use it.

## Zoom to selection and frames

- **Zoom to selection and frames:** `centerOrigin` (the canvas context menu's "Center on origin (0, 0)", `engine.centerOrigin()`) glides the view to the world's origin at its zoom. `zoomToSelection` (Shift+2) fits the selection (`engine.zoomToSelection()`, past 100% when it's small: `Camera.fitRect(rect, { padding, maxZoom })`, `FitOptions`; no selection: zoom to fit). `nextFrame`/`previousFrame` (N / Shift+N, `engine.zoomToFrame(1 | -1)`) fit and select the next or previous of the product's `frames(elements)` hook (one module; the design's top-level visible containers, `topFrames`; the board's sections, `boardSections`; both in `readingOrder`, `canvas/arrange/reading-order.ts`, rows as tidy up makes them): after the selected one or the one holding the selection (`withMembers(…, "move")`), else the one nearest the middle of the view, wrapping round. In the `ZoomMenu`, the selection's context menu and the design's View menu.

## Copy and paste properties

- **Copy and paste properties** (Figma's, Ctrl+Alt+C / Ctrl+Alt+V, matched by `code`): a kind lists the stored fields that make up its look in `styleFields`; `styleOf(el)` copies them (`ElementStyle`, plain JSON) and `applyStyle(el, style)` patches the ones it has too, matched **by name across types** (a rectangle's fills paste onto a text). `engine.copyStyle()` takes the first selected element's into a per-product slot that lives for the app session (`canvas/clipboard/style-clipboard.ts`, never the system clipboard; `hasCopiedStyle()`), `engine.pasteStyle(ids?)` applies it to the unlocked selection in one undo step and `engine.applyStyleOf(fromId, ids)` does the same from an element (agents' `copy_properties_to_nodes`/`copy_properties_to_shapes`, `canvas/mcp/style.mcp.ts`, part of `arrangeTools`). The design's nodes take `opacity`, `fills`, `strokes`, `strokeWeight` (`NODE_STYLE_FIELDS`; `nodeStylePatch` drops fills where `fillable` is off, strokes where `strokable` is, image paints from strokes and kinds without `imageFills`), rectangles and frames `cornerRadius`, texts their typography and alignment (set again with `retypeset`), lines their caps; groups nothing. The board's stickies, tables and sections their `color`, shapes `fill`, `stroke`, `strokeWidth`, `fontSize`, texts `color` and `fontSize` (sized to their text again), strokes `color`, `size`, `pen`, connectors `color`, `size`, `style` and heads, images `background` and `padding` (`imagePaddingPatch`); DB and ER elements nothing. In the selection's context menu and the design's Edit menu.

## Rotation

- **Rotation** (Figma's model): `CanvasElement.rotation` is degrees counterclockwise round the element's top-left corner (`x`, `y`); `w`×`h` is its unturned size, so growing from the corner (text, vector re-wrapping, right/bottom resizes) needs no correction. Kinds opt in with `ElementKind.rotatable` (the design's nodes; board kinds don't) and turn with `kind.rotate(el, degrees, pivot)` (the corner orbits, `rotation` adds up; the design's line turns its ends instead and keeps 0). Kinds work unturned: `Scene.elementAt` and `findInteraction` hand them `toUnrotated(p, el)`, `ElementView` places, turns and culls the view (`rotation.ts`: `rotatePoint`, `rotatedBounds`, `rotatedBoundsAround`, `pointInRotatedRect`, `normalizeAngle`, `angleOf`). `elementBounds(kind, el)` is where an element shows (the box round it when turned): hits, marquees, snapping, arranging, exports, thumbnails and paste placement read it; resizing reads `kind.bounds`. `ElementClip.rotation` turns a clip (masks are drawn in the view's frame, `clipCovers` checks in the clip's; `clipBounds`). The overlay draws `Outline`s (rect + rotation); a single turned element's resize handles sit on its turned box (`ResizeTarget.rotation`, `resizeHandlePoint`/`resizeHandleAt`/`resizeCursor` take it) and `ResizeGesture.resizeTurned` resizes it in its own frame; in a multi-resize turned elements follow by their center. A press just outside a corner of the selection's target when everything in it turns (`rotateZoneAt`, `ResizeTarget.resizable` false for turn-only targets like a group) starts a `RotateGesture` (round the target's center, Shift: 15° steps, the angle shown with `SnapFeedback.labels`, the `rotateCursor`). `engine.rotateElements(ids, degrees | (el) => degrees, "selection" | "each")` and the `rotateLeft`/`rotateRight` commands (no keys) turn with members in one undo step. The text editor turns its textarea. **Mirroring:** `CanvasElement.mirrored` draws an element mirrored left to right inside its own box, before its turn: `ElementView` scales by −1 round its box (its extent and clip masks follow), `toUnrotated` un-mirrors hit points too, and `placePath`/`localPath` take `mirrored` and `w` in their `PathPlacement` (`mirrorPath` mirrors a local shape); its box, bounds and handles don't change. The rich text editor edits a mirrored text mirrored.

## Commands and shortcuts

- **Commands and shortcuts:** every engine action is a command (`commands.ts`), every tool gets `tool.<id>`, and each of the product's `hostCommands` (e.g. the board's `openLibrary`) runs the handler the host registered with `engine.handleHostAction(id, handler)`. **A product's `keybindings` are the single source for shortcuts**: `BASE_KEYBINDINGS` (`input/base-keybindings.ts`) plus the `keys` its modules give their tools and host commands. The engine wraps them in a `Keymap`; the UI reads labels with `engine.keymap.label(command)`, so never hard-code shortcut strings in components. Key matching and formatting helpers live in `shared/core/keyboard.ts` (shared with the `useHotkey` hook in `shared/ui/hooks/`).

## UI state

- **UI state** is exposed as an immutable `EngineState` snapshot (`utils/store.ts`, shallow-compared), via `engine.subscribe` / `engine.getState`.
