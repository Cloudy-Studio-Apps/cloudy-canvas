# Rulers and ruler guides

_How it works. The rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md); names may still follow the pre-refactor conventions until their phase (see its Transition table)._

## Rulers and ruler guides

- **Rulers and ruler guides** (`canvas/ruler-guides/`): a product that keeps guides gives the `guides` hook (`CanvasGuides`: `read(doc, scope)`/`write(doc, scope, guides)`, one module at most; the design keeps them on each page's record). A `RulerGuide` is `{ axis, value, frame? }` (on `x` a vertical line): a page guide crosses the whole canvas at `value`; a frame's guide (`frame`, an element id) counts from that element's corner, shows only inside it and moves with it. `placeGuides` turns them into `PlacedGuide`s (world value, `within` the frame's unturned bounds and its `rotation`; guides of a gone frame are left out), `guideInside` says whether one crosses its frame, and a turned frame's guides turn with it (`frameGuideEnds`, hit through `inGuideFrame`, dragged along the frame's axes, never snapped to). The engine reads them once per document change (`ScopeGuides`, `engine.placedGuides()`): `engine.guides()`, `setGuides`/`addGuides`/`removeGuides(indexes?)` (one undo step each), `setGuidesShown` and `setRulersShown` (`EngineState.guidesShown`/`rulersShown`). `render/layers/ruler-guide-layer.ts` draws them as 1px filled rects in the snap color (across the view, or across their frame), with the dragged one's value (a frame's from its corner) by its ruler. They're snap targets (`SnapSettings.guides`: edges without marks or boxes, `snapTargets(boxes, lines)`), and a press within 3 px of one (inside its frame; after handles, element interactions and rotate zones) is the `guide` interaction: the select tool drives a `GuideDrag` (`tools/guide-drag.ts`: snaps with `Snapper.line`, not to itself, `rulerGuideKey`; a frame's guide stays the frame's while it crosses it and becomes a page guide past its edges; dropped on its ruler, `overRuler` within `RULER_SIZE` = 20 px while the rulers show, it's removed). The rulers are DOM (`canvas-ui` `Rulers`, `components/rulers/`): two `<canvas>` strips over the canvas's top and left edges (`drawRuler`, steps of 1/2/5 × 10ⁿ from `rulerStep(zoom)`, the selection's extent from `engine.selectionBounds()` with its values, which hide the numbers they'd overlap, colors from `--canvas-ruler*`), measuring `engine.rulerFrame()` (the product's `rulerFrame(selected, elements)` hook, one module, returns the element; the design's `selectionRulerFrame`: the container the selection shares, or a selected top-level container): they count from its corner and number only its span (the page's while it's turned), and a guide dragged out of them meanwhile is its guide (in a turned frame along its axis closest to the ruler's, `screenAxis`) (`engine.beginGuideDrag(axis, event)` returns a `GuideDragHandle`). They redraw on `onViewChange` and selection changes. `CanvasViewOptions.rulers` says a product shows them (the design's).
