# Rendering

_How it works. The rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md); names may still follow the pre-refactor conventions until their phase (see its Transition table)._

## Masks

- **Masks** (Figma's, `render/layer-placement.types.ts`): the product's `masks(elements)` hook (one module) returns `ElementMasks`: each `ElementMask` (`id`, `mode`: `alpha`/`vector`/`luminance`, `inverted`, `home`: the `MaskSlot` its scope sits in, `bounds`, `shape`: world-placed `MaskShape`s (`path`, drawn with holes) for a vector mask or `null`, `contains(p)`) and `homes` (element → its innermost `MaskSlot`: `{ mask, role: "source" | "masked" }`). The scene keeps them (`Scene.masks`, `masksOf(el)`, a changed slot is in `replacedIds`); `elementAt` skips what a mask hides, `shownBounds(el)` (bounds ∩ clip ∩ the masks it shows through) is what marquees, snapping, exports and thumbnails read, and the overlay outlines the hovered (or single selected) element's masks dashed (`OverlayState.masks`). `ElementLayer` nests views: each mask is a `MaskScopeView` (in its home's container at the mask's order key) holding a `MaskSource` (the mask's views, never drawn on their own: rendered into the mask's texture, an `AlphaMask` through the `MaskSourceEffect` Pixi extension) and the masked views; `luminance` puts brightness in the red channel with a `ColorMatrixFilter`, `vector` is a stencil of the outlines (or the source made opaque when a part has none), `inverted` is Pixi's inverse mask. A scope off screen is culled and its texture covers only what's on screen (`boundsArea`).

## Rendering

- **Rendering:** `ElementView` (`canvas/render/element-view.ts`) is the one Pixi container for every element. `sync(el, kind)` calls `kind.draw(view, el)`, and `view.placeText(text, style, w, h)` lays text out like the editor. `view.setPaints(layers, { alpha, visible })` draws a stack of paints above the base graphics (`PaintStack`, `render/paint-stack.ts`): `shape` layers (a fill or stroke traced into a `Graphics`; `draw(g, tools)`, where `tools.gradient(gradient, box, alpha)` is a fill style of a `Gradient` laid on a box: its texture, `render/gradient-texture.ts`, is drawn once per gradient (512² over the box's normalized area with a margin, mirrored past it, a 1024-color ramp (`gradientRamp`), dithered) and shared while views use it) and `image` layers (an asset fitted with `contain`/`cover`, `render/fit-image.ts`, masked by an outline), so images can sit between fills and under strokes. `view.setPaintTextMask(true)` shows the paints only through the view's text (drawn white; `render/text-mask.ts` draws its glyphs with Pixi's `CanvasTextGenerator` into an alpha mask) instead of the text itself. Nothing outside the element folders knows how an element looks.
